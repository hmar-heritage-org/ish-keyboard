Ram pakhat chu tu khom hriet fie thei dinga chieng konga ei ṭawngkam indik hmanga thu an hrilna a nih. A dang chu thu a chieng naw thei dan taka her kawia ṭawngkam indik loa hrilna ram a nih. A hmasa hi Chienga ram a na, a nuhnung hi Buoia ram a nih. Ei ditthlangna zirin ei ngirhmun inthlak tung pei a tih.
