# Trong Humhal

* **Date:** `2009-03-08`

* **Hmar Ratio:** `0.85`

---

If thought corrupts language, language also

can corrupt thought.

- George Orwell

Ṭawng hi Pathien pek a ni nawh. Mihriemin ei thil hmu, hriet le ngaituohai hrilna dinga ri hrang hrang hmanga ei ser suok le sukpung pei a nih. A hmangtuhaiin hma an sawn ang peiin hma a sawn a, an hnung tol chun a hnung tol a, an boral chun a boral ve el a nih. Kum sawmnga sung chauin ṭawng ei inhmang der thei. Entirna ṭha tak pakhat chu, Babulon sala intang Israelhai kha a nih. Sawt naw teah Hebrai ṭawng theinghilin Aramik ṭawng an hmang vong hman a nih. Isu hun laia an ṭawng hmang khom kha Hebrai ṭawng ni loin Aramik ṭawng a ni tah lem.

‘Hmar ṭawng’ tia ko tah hi Mizorama Lusei lalhai hnuoia khawsa Hmar hnathlak pahnam hrang hrang ṭawng le Dulien (Lusei) ṭawng inchekpola inthoka ṭawng thar hung suok a nih. Hmar hnathlak laia thlang lo tla hmasahai chun ‘Khawsak Ṭawng’ tiin an ko. A hnunga Lusei lalhai opna hnuoia inthoka suok dok Hmar hnathlakhaiin pahnam hming senga inko neka an renga huop zo hminga an ngai ‘Hmar’ ti hi inkona tlanglawna an hung hmang hnungin, hi ‘Khawsak Ṭawng’ hi ‘Hmar Ṭawng’ tia ko a hung ni ta pei a nih. Kristien sakhuo a hung lut hnungin, hi ṭawng hi Gospel thu le hla puongdarna ṭawnga hmang a hung ni pei leiin Hmar pahnam ṭawnghai laia ṭawng tlanglawn tak le hmang takah a hung ṭhang tah a nih.

Mi tam tak chun ‘Hmar’ ti hming hi ‘pi le pu chen khoma an lo sak sa,’ tiena tlanga inthoka lo um tah, Hmar ṭawng khom chuong ang thoa upa ni dingin ei pawng ring a, “A tirin thu a um” ti leh ruoltlakah ei sie nuom hiel a nih. Tienami phuok thiemhai lem chun Hmarhai hi Mosie kaiza vengtuhai lai inṭhangtirin, Tuipui Sen ei inkantir a, Sinai thlalerah nghok pawt khopin kum sawmli zet ‘mana’ ei inringtir a, ‘mana’ khom chu Hmar ṭawnga ‘Iemana?” tia inthoka suok ni dingin ei hril hiel a nih. Chuonghai lai chun pastor khom ei ṭhang nok a nih. Sienkhom, histawri ei hang sui chieng a, kum 1800 hnunga ‘Hmar’ ti hnam hming hi hung pieng chauh a ni bok si. Zo hnathlak hnam hrang hrang Ralte (Galte), Paite, Pawi (Lai), Lakher (Mara) le a dang dang inkona hminghai hi Chin Hills-a ei khawsak hnung daia Dulien (Lusei) ṭawng hmanghaiin an mi kona hming ei som deu vong a nih.

Kum 1200 AD vela Kabaw phairuom ei hung zuon lai le a hma khan ieng hnam hming am ei put ti le ieng ṭawng tlanglawn am ei hmang ti hi chieng taka sui thei ei la um nawh. Hnam tinin tienami anga ei dong sawng chanchin ruok chu hril ding ei nei a, a upa lem nina inchu khom ei nuom seng bok. Amiruokchu, tuta ei hming chawi ang chun a tu khom hi hnam upa ei um nawh. Chin Hills-a ei um laiin Kawlhaiin ‘Chin’ tiin an mi ko a. Thlang tla hmasa pawlin Korvai (Bengalihai) ei zuk paw khan ‘Kuki’ tiin an mi lo ko nawk a. A hnung daiah, Sailo lalhaiin an del chin rama umhai le Dulien ṭawng hmanghai po kha ‘Lusei’ tiin an ko a. British hai an hung khan eini Zo hnathlak hmun hrang hranga umhai chu Chin, Kuki le Lushai tiin an mi hung hriet tah a nih. ‘Hmar’ ti hming lem hi chu lekhabua a chuongna hmasa tak chu 1900 khan a ni chauh.

Chuong chu a ni lai zingin, Hmar hming a pieng hma daiin pahnam hrang hrang hming chu ei lo nei dai tah. Pahnam le a tesep hminga ei khaw hluo, ei hnam hming hrang hrang ei inputtir khuo iemani zat chu Champhai le a se velah tu chen hin an la ngir zing. Hriet dinga poimaw chu, Ṭipura, NC Hills, Cachar le Manipur a cheng, Old Kuki ti hnuoia an sie Hmar hnathlak tam lem hi ‘Hmar’ hming a pieng hmaa thlang tla le Mizorama inthoka inpem suok hman tah vong an nih. Chuleiin, anni Old Kuki hnuoia pahnam hrang hrang hai hi Hmar ṭawng awrizinal (original) neitu an nih. Hmar ṭawng humhal thu ei hril ding chun anni hi ei ngai poimaw hmasa ding chu an nih, a ṭawng bul neitu le hmangtu an ni tlat leiin. Old Kuki hnuoia Linguistic Survey of India (1904) in a siehai chu Anal, Aimuol, Biete, Chawthe, Chawrai, Chiru, Hmar, Hallam, Hrangkhol, Kolhren (Koireng) Kom, Lamjang (Lamkang), Purum, Ranglong (Langrong) le a dang dang an nih.

Kum 1900 laia British sorkarin linguistic survey an thaw khan ‘Hmar ṭawng’ hmang chu mi 2000 chauh rikawt an la ni laiin Hrangkhol ṭawng hmang 7,820, Hallam ṭawng hmang 26,848 le Ranglong ṭawng hmang 6,266 an um dai tah a nih (Linguistic Survey of India Vol 3 Part 3 p.181). Hi hin a hmaa ka hril tah kha chieng takin a hung namdet a nih. Sienkhom, ‘Hmar ṭawng’ ei ti hin ram a lak hrat êm êm a, kum sawmnga sung vel chauin Hmar hnathlakhai laia ṭawng tlanglawn tak a hung ni hman bakah tu hin chu College chena Vernacular subject (MIL) pakhatah a hung ṭhang sa ve hman tah a nih.

Ieng leia chuong lawma hung inṭhang hrat am ning a ta? Lusei (Dulien) ṭawng le Hmar pahnam ṭawng hrang hrang inchekpola ṭawng thar hung suok a ni leiin Hmar hnathlak pahnam tu khomin hriet olsam an ti el bakah kâr loin an thiem thei nghal lei a nih. Pahnina, Gospel puongdarna ṭawnga hmang, Baibul inletna ṭawng, thu le hla thar a hung luong suokna ṭawng, Mission sikul tina inchuktirna ṭawng a hung a ni lei. Chîk taka ei bi chun, kum 1919 vela ‘Hmar ṭawng’ hmanga huoisen taka Pastor Thangngurin hla a hung phuoka inthokin hi ṭawng hin zung a hung kei tak tak chauh a nih. Hi ra suok poimaw hmu ding pieng hmasa tak chu kum 1923-a Hmar ṭawnga hlabu hmasa tak an sut kha a nih. Chu hnung zuitu chu Pastor Thangngur thi hnung kum thumna, April 1946-a hung suok, Dr. Thanglung buotsai Hmar ṭawnga Thuthlung Thar bu hmasa tak kha a nih.

Ṭawng hi vadung ang a ni a, a chawmtu hnâr hrang hrang luong khawmin a siem a nih. Hmar ṭawng chawmtu hnâr hrang hrang hai chu Hmar hnathlak pahnam ṭawng hrang hrang dam, Lusei (Mizo) ṭawng dam, ni tina ei indawrpui ei kawl le kienga um hnam danghai ṭawng ei lak lut dam le lekha inchukna le fakzongnaa ei ṭawng hmang, abikin Sapṭawng dam an nih. A ṭhen chu ei hriet naw kara ei lo hmang ṭhang tah le eini ta renga ei ngai tah an ni a, a ṭhen ruok chu hre zinga ei ṭawng sukhausakna ding le a baksamna phuhruktu dinga ei lak lut an nih. Chu bakah ei hmasawn ang peiin ei ngirhmun thara dingin ṭawngkam thar ei mamaw a, ei ser thar pei bok a. Baibul (Delhi Version) kan buotsai ṭan hlimin thu chabi poimaw pakhat, entirnan ‘worship’ ti hi Hmar ṭawngin a um naw a, Zo hnathlak hnam dang a nei an um am tiin ka dap a, tu khom a nei an um nawh ti ka hmu suok a. Chuleiin, ‘chawibiek’ ti ṭawngkam thar hi, Pathien chawimawia biek (worship) hrilna dingin ka ser a. Delhi tlanga chu hi thu mal ṭhang lo hin seramu kan thùr thei ta nawh. Keima ringot khoma chuong ang ṭawngkam thar ka ser chu iemani zat a um a, Zoram khawvela thu mal hmang lar pawl an hung ni tah pei a nih.

Hnam dang ṭawng thiem taka lak lut hi ṭawng sukhausakna dinga thil ṭha a ni lai zingin, lak lut dan thiem naw chun mani ṭawng inchim raltir dèr thei a nih. Sapṭawng hmangtu English mihai chun an ram hluo le op taphota mihai ṭawng, anni ṭawnga an nei naw le an nei zing khom an ṭawnga lak lut dan an thiem a, an lak lut taphot chu an ṭawng awphawiin an ziek vong bok a. Hi lei hin, khawvela ṭawng hmangruo hau tak an hung ni thei a nih. French hai ruok chu insukfirin hnam dang ṭawng lak lut an nuom naw a, an ṭawng khawvel ṭawng ni thei ding kha ni thei loin a um pha a nih. English-haiin an ṭawng hlui (Old English) thu mal 50000-60000 vel chauha inthokin 988,968 zeta tam an sukpung laiin, French hai chun thu mal hmang tlanglawn 186,674 chauh an nei a nih.

Hnam dang ṭawng lak lut thuah hriet ding poimaw pahnih a um. Pakhatna, ei ṭawng hi mal tin umzie nei (monosyllabic) le thu mal pangngai char khom ei thluk dan zira kawk le umzie hrang hrang nei (phonetic) a ni leiin, chuong ang nei ve hnamhai ṭawnga inthoka lak chu a him el bakah thu mal hrang hrang siemna dingin a ṭangkai. Kong danga hril chun, ei ṭawng le inzawl ṭawnga inthoka thu mal ei haw chun tê tam tak a hring thei leiin ṭawng sukhausakna hnâr poimaw a ni thei. Chun, Zo hnathlak hrang hrang ṭawng laia a ieng khom hi hmang inla, hnam dang ṭawng ei haw ni loin eini ṭawng hnâr hrima inthoka suok, ei lo la hmang ngai lo amanih le ei hmang zui ta lo amanih kha ei hmang nawk el a nih. Chu chu Hmar ṭawng le Lusei ṭawng hmang pol thuah a ni zuol. Meitei ṭawng khom la inla, ei ṭawng hnara inthoka suok tho a nih. Chuong ang bokin, Chinese (Mandarin), Kawl (Burmese) le Tibetan ṭawnghai khom.

Amiruokchu, eini ṭawng zie le inang lo, entirnan, Indo-Europeon ṭawng hieng Sanskrit, German, English le a dang danga inthoka ei haw ruok chu ei ṭawng phung le inang lo a ni leiin thu mal ei haw char kha hmang thei a na, thu mal dang siemna hmangruoa hmang thei a ni nawh. Sapṭawng mal ei haw hrim hrim, entirnan school, subject, exam, pen, pass, fail, vote, candidate le a dang dang haia inthok hin thu mal dang siem belsa thei a ni nawh. Chuleiin, lo thei lo a ni ngot naw chun hieng ang ṭawng haw hi fimkhur a ngai.

Chun, Sapṭawng amanih hnam dang ṭawng hrim hrim ei haw hin a ṭawng neituhaiin an ziek dan anga ziek am, an naw leh eini ṭawng awphawia ziek lem ding ti thuah ngaidan a khuongruol nawh. English-hai chun mi ṭawnga inthoka an lakhai chu ieng thu mal khom ni sien, hming khom ni sien, anni awphawi le inmil le lo thei loa an ngaihai chauh naw chu anni awphawiin an ziek deu vong. Entirnan, Mumbai chu Bombay, Kolkata chu Calcutta, Aizawl chu Aijal, Venezia chu Venice, Firenze chu Florence, Isa chu Jesus, Ibrahim chu Abraham, Yacub chu Jacob, Yangon chu Rangoon, Varanasi chu Benares etc. Italian hai, Indonesian hai, Malaysian hai le hnam dang dang khomin ṭawng dang an haw hi anni awphawiin an ziek vong bok.

Chu zai chu zui vein, lo thei loa Sapṭawng ei hawhai hi eini ṭawng thlukin ka ziek vong a nih: erpawt (airport), ekzam (exam), futbawl (football), farmasi (pharmacy), Baibul (Bible). A san chu, ei hawrop hmanga thu ziek sia Sapṭawnga an ziek dan anga ei ziek dai chun a lamrik dan indik thei ta lo hrim hrim a ni bakah a ruolin alphabet lam dan inang lo hmang kop ei tum a ni leiin thil thaw awm a ni thei naw a, ei ṭawng le a lam dan a chokchawrawi vong thei a nih. Ei thaw ding a ni law law chun ei A AW B hi bàn a, Sapṭawnga A B C hi ei hmang lem ngai a tih. Chu chu ei ṭawng ziekna dinga hmang thei a ni si naw leiin a hranin A AW B an mi lo siempek a nih ti hi hriet zing ding a nih. Chu chu a ni lai zingin, ei alphabet hmang lai hi ei ṭawng khom an la thiem hmaa misawnarihaiin Hunterian system hmanga inhmaw taka an mi lo siempek ei hmang zui pei a ni a, duthu a sàm naw a, siem ṭhat a ngai bakah thlûk sinsiena chi hrang hrang, a tlawm takah panga bêk ei siem a ngai.

G.A.Grierson-in Linguistic Survey of India a buotsai lai khan eini rawi Meitei, Chin-Kuki-Mizo pawl, Chin Hills, Arakan, Manipur, Assam, Ṭipura le Chittagong Hills Tract-a umhai hi bawm khat, Tibeto-Burman Family, Kuki-Chin & Burma Groups sungah a mi khum khawm vong a, sul khat suok ei ni vong khom ring a um. Hi Group sunga hin R hmang pawl le G hmang pawlin ei inṭhe nawk a. Ieng huna inṭhe am ei lo ni tah ti hi thil sui mihaiin an la hung sui suok ngei beisei inla. Chin Hills ei hung chuongkai hnunga thil tlung a ni ngei kei chun ka ring. Tlang hrang hrang ei hung hluo hnungin ei ṭawng a hung danglam tiel tiel a, hun sawt naw te sungin ṭawng chi hrang hrang hmang 47 lai ei suok hman a nih.

Hun sawt naw te sung bokin Sailo lalhai chun an inlalna ram sunga um po chu ṭawng khat hmang hnamin an siem a, chu chun Mizoram chauh ni lo, Mizo hnam le Mizo ṭawng khom a hring suok tah pei a nih. Mizoram sunga Zo hnathlak hnam hrang hrangin lien lem le hrat lema insuikhawmna dinga hnam khat hnuoia ṭawng khat hmanga zai khata an luong hi hnam varhai thaw dan le hnama dam khawsuokna chabi a nih. Chuleiin, Raltehaiin an ṭawng inhmanga ei hril vet hi hnam dam khawsuokna dinga lien lema Zo tuipuia insung khawm nachang an hriet leia thil tlung a ni leiin hnam var le dawn sei an nina lemah kei chun ka hriet. Vadung lien lem fin an ni leiin an var man an hmu pei hrim a nih.

Sienkhom, Raltehai anga thaw nachang hre lo tam tak ei la um a. Chuonghai chun thei le thei loin, harsa takin ei ṭawng bing seng sukhring le sukhrat tumin kawng kul rak khopin ei bei a, ei sawlpui hle a, sienkhom hma ei sawn maw hle. A tawpa chu, ieng po khom thawin hril inla, thu le hlaa ei chak lakna hnâr tak chu Zo tuipui a ni tho tho. Dulien ṭawnga inthokin Lusei ṭawng, Lusei ṭawng le Zo hnathlak ṭawng hrang hrang infawk khawma inthokin Zoṭawng a hung insiem tung mek a. Zo hnathlak hnam hrang hrang thu le hla le ṭawng hai hi luong khawm pei an ta, kum zanga sungin Zoṭawng hausa tak la hung suok ngei a tih. Ram hrang hranga um Sapṭawnga thu le hla phusuoktuhai chu English literature sukhausatu an ni ang bokin, tlang hrang hranga cheng Zo hnathlakhai thu le hla phusuokhai chu Zo Literature sukhausatu an la hung ni ve vong ding a nih. Thu le hla ka ziekhai po po hi Zo bawmpuia luong lut dinga ka duong vong a nih.

Hma ei sawn ang peiin ṭawng khom a hung inthlak a, ei hmang ta nawhai kha sirah ei hnawl a, an thi pei a, a tharin an hung thlak a. Chemtum le tuthlaw hmanga fak zongtuin pen le computer hmanga fak a zong hnung chun kha hmaa a fakzongnaa ṭawngkam a hmanghai kha hmang thei a ni ta naw leiin a hluihlawn a, a hmang naw song chun a bo song el a nih. Chuonga bo song ding ṭawngkam hum dan ṭha tak chu lekhabua hlu lut le hma ei sawn tung ang peia umzie thar inputtira som tung pei a nih. Chu chu hnam var le hmasawnhai thaw dan a nih. Entirnan, tien lai chu mi neinunghaiin malsawmna an dong lawmnain khawtlang hraiin ruoi an ṭhe hlak a, chu chu ‘inchong’ an tih. Kristien ei hung ni hnungin inchong hi ei thaw tah ngai nawh. Delhi tlanga ruok chu, thil thar nei amanih lawmthu hrilna ding neihaiin Hmar Christian Fellowship inkhawm ṭinah fak ding bei le dawn ding thingpui buotsaiin inkhawmhai po po an khilai hlak a, chu chu ‘inchong’ an tih.

Ṭawng hi a neitu le hmangtuhai dung le khang neka sei le liena inṭhang thei der lo a nih. A mihriemin hma sawn si loa ei ṭawngin hma sawn dinga beisei chu beiseina thlawn a nih. Seminar le inkhawmpui vela thu ropui tak tak rela sukhmasawn el thei khom a ni nawh. Ṭawng sukhmasawntu laia poimaw chu chanchinbu mihai le lekha ziektuhai an nih. Sienkhom ei chanchinbuhai hi eini mi tlawmte khom huop zo phak loa ei sukkholbing tawl an ni leiin a dam sawt muol an um thei nawh. Ei puon in zauh lien a ngai. Ei zauh lien ang ruol peiin ei thu le hla mi dang khomin hung inhnikin tiem veng an ta, ei chanchinbuhai lien tuol tuol a ta, ei ṭawng khom inzar lien deu deu a tih. Chu chu ṭawng sukhmasawnna le humhalna dinga a kila lung ṭhut poimaw pakhat a nih. Huop chin hi mani le mani inuop hlumna a nih.

A tawp taka chun, tu laia ṭawng ei suosam nasatna tak chu kohran buzawl le biek in a nih. Ei pulpithai hi ṬAWNGTHATZAWL NAMBAR 1 an nih. Kum tam ka sui hnungin, ṭawng indik lo ei inchuk rawnna tak khom a mimira ei tiem rawn tak ei Baibul niin ka hriet. Chu taka inthoka ṭawng hmang dan indik lo ei lak chun a mi dawi vet hnê em leiin ṭawng indik lo ei hmang a nih ti khom ei hriet tah hrim hrim nawh. Ei ṭawng san suok ding chun Hmar ṭawng fe dan indika inlet Baibul ei mamaw hmasa tak nia hriet leiin Delhi Version hi a hung suok a nih. Chu chu a ring naw ei um chun lungril kotkhar hongin, chîk takin a hmasa leh enmilin inla, hrilfietu dang ei ngai ka ring nawh. Ei la ngai rop a ni chun san suok ruol lo khopa chawlolin ei ngaituona a lo piengsuol tah a ni el thei.

(March 8, 2009 Delhi)
