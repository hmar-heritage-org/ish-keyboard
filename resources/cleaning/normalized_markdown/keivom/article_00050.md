# Lekhabu Huoisen Tak

The Bible is no mere book, but a Living Creature,

with a power that conquers all that oppose it.

- Napoleon Bonaparte

1992 kum bula muol lo liem tah ka ruolpa Prof. Siamkima Khawlhring chun a lekhabu ZALENNA RAM ah Baibul hi khawvela lekhabu huoisen tak a nizie thu a ziek a. Hi hi thudik muhlum a nih. A huoisen tak chauh khom ni loin huvang nei tak, thilthawthei tak a ni bok. Hi lekhabu thu le inzom thu chuongna lekhabu le thu ziek hrim hrim raw hmang vong ding ni inla, khawvela laibrari po po deuthaw hi ruok deu huoi a tih. Hi lekhabu le inzoma hrietna, thiemna le kong hrang hranga hmasawnna po po hi bo vong tah sien, khawvel hi kum za tam tak hnung kir a ta, selfa hnamah inchang nawk mei ei tih. Dannaranin, a hun laia lekhabu ṭhaa hril thang khom hi sut a nia inthoka kum sawmnga hnunga tiem hlaw chu tlawmte chauh a ni hlak. Kum za hnunga tiem hlaw lekhabu lem chu a tlawm zuol. Baibul ruok chu kum 1500 vel sunga mi hrang hrang ziek lak khawm ni sien khom lekhabu hlawm khat, pat hlum ni sien, zai inzom vong a ni tlat lei hin Tirko Paula chun, “Pathien inthuok khum” a ni thu a ziek a ( 2 Tim 3:16). Isu zirtir laia ri se tak le phok tak Peter khomin chu chu mihriemin Thlarau Thienghlim ṭhuoia Pathiena inthoka suok an puong a ni thu a ziek bok a nih (2 Pet 1 :20-21). Chuleiin, an sut rawn deu deu a, tiem a hlaw deu deu bok.

Baibul hi, Thuthlung Hlui tawp tienga Aramik ṭawnga ziek tlawmte a lo inther sa ti lo chu, Hebrai ṭawng le Grik ṭawnga ziek a nih. Thuthlung Hlui tieng po hi Pathienin a thuthlung hlen suokna dinga hmangruoa a thlang Abraham le a thlahai ṭawng hmang, a hnunga ‘Hebrai ṭawng’ tia hrieta ziek a nih. Anni tak ruok chun ‘Hebrai mi’ ti hi hnam hmingah hmang ngai awm nawh; hnam dangin an hmusit kona ni lemin a’n lang. Tu laia Israel State khom hin ‘Hebrai ṭawng’ ti hi a pom a, ‘Hebrai mi’ ti ruok hi chu hnam hmingah a pom nawh. Hi ṭawng hi Eufretis le Tigris vadungpui inkar le a se vela chenghai ṭawng hmang Aramik leh ṭawng inhnai tak a nih. Babulon sala Israelhai intang hung suok khan Hebrai ṭawng nekin Aramik ṭawng an hmang tlanglawn tah lem a, Isu hun laia an ṭawng hmang khom Hebrai ṭawng ni loin Aramik ṭawng a nih. Tuta an Hebrai ṭawng hmang, ‘Modern Hebrew’ hi Hebrai ṭawng hlui leh inzula kum 1900 hnunga ṭawng thar hung pieng a nih.

Hi thu hi eini tonhriet le inzomin hang hril sap met ei tih. Tuta ‘Hmar ṭawng’ ei ti tak khom hi Hmar pahnam hrang hrang ṭawng le Dulien/Lusei ṭawng inchekpola inthoka kum 1700 hnunga ṭawng thar hung pieng, thlang tla hmasahaiin ‘Khawsak ṭawng’ an ti, kristien sakhuo a hung lut hnunga Hmar pahnam ṭawng hrangin ṭawng tlanglawna an hmang thei dinga ṭawng hung ṭal thut a nih. Chu ‘Khawsak ṭawng’ chun unau suopui, a hlawma inkona tlanglawn nei loa pahnam hrang hranga inko hlakhai chu hnam khatah hui khawmin, an renga inkona dingin ‘HMAR’ ti hnam hming hi a hung hring suok bok a nih. Chuong zawnga sui chun, ‘Hmar hnam’ le ‘Hmar ṭawng’ ei ti hi Baibul kalchawina dinga a lampui lo sat lawk tia hril thei a nih. Chuong anga Zo hnathlakhai laia ṭawng tlanglawna hmang dinga chanchin ṭhain a lo duong lawk chu Dulien ṭawng hi a nih. Dulien ṭawngin Lusei ṭawng a hring a, Lusei ṭawngin Mizo ṭawng.

Chu thu le inzoma ei hril nuom tak chu lekhabu huoisen thilthawthei dan a nih. Hi lekhabu hi, Prof. Siamkima ti dan takah, chechang loa um ṭawk ṭawk thei lo a ni a, do nei zing ngirhmunah a ngir a, a va nèkhai chu chungdelh a tum ngei ngei a, an do nasat po leh a puok dar nasa el a nih. A khingpui Koran hai, Gita hai le sakhuo lekhabu dang danghai chu chet hmasa tum loa inveng ringot angin an um thung a nih. Hi lekhabu ropui bok hi Rom sorkar ropui tak le an lal Constantine chopa hnetu a ni a, Rom sorkar hmang hin British-hai khom a thu hnuoiah a lo sie bok a nih. British thlierkar chikte, tu hriet hlaw mang lo chu a thilthawtheinain a vawr tung a, hun sawt nawte sungin ram ropui le thilthawtheiin a’n din suok a, hnuoi hmun thuma ṭhea hmun hni zet a thu hnuoiah ramperin (colony) a siem a, an ram lakna taphotah an hnam puonzar (flag) zuiin Baibul a lut a, chu chun an ṭawng khom kum zahni vel sung chauin khawvela ṭawng inlâr tak le hmang tlanglawn takin a vor kai dot el a nih. Voisun hin Sapṭawng ṭhangna taphot lekhabu le thil ziek po po lak hmang vong ding ti thu um sien, khawvel hi mutpuom inthla thêp ang ning a ta, thil siemna khawl ropui po po deuthaw chawl a ta, khawvel hi chechang loin êk che inṭhung ringot a tih.

Khawvela hnam var hmasa le literechar tienga hmaṭhuoituhai chu Grik mi an nih. Anni hi thu le hlaa khawthlang mihai chàk lakna tak an nih. Chu ṭawng chu Thuthlung Thar ziekna ṭawng a nih. Chu zoa Baibul an hung inletna ṭawng chu khawvel histawria lalram ngir sawt tak Rom lalram ṭawng hmang Latin a nih. Jerusalema inthokin hi lekhabu huoisen hin Rom-ah a hmunpui a son a, Rom lalram cho dolin a thu hnuoiah a sie a. Iemani chen hnungah Grik ṭawng hmang pawl le Latin ṭawng hmang pawlin an hung inṭhe dar a. Grik ṭawng hmang pawlhai hmunpui dingin Kumpinu Constantine chun Turkey rama an khawpui chu a hmang a, ama hming chawiin, Constantinople tiin hming thar a phuok a, chu chu tuta Istanbul ei ti hi a nih. Hi khawsak tienga Rom lalram hi a nih Byzantine Empire an ti, Eastern Roman Empire tia an ko bok chuh. Thlang tieng Western Roman Empire chun Rom khawpui chu hmunpuiah a hmang zing a, a hnungah Roman Catholic hmunpui hung niin, tu chen hin a hung ni zui tah pei a nih.

Rom lalram tluk hnungin lekhabu huoisen chun hmunpuiah London a hung hmang a. Chu chu hmanga khawvel hmun tin a rùn hnungin a mi hmanghaiin anni varna le theina sawnin damten lekhabu huoisen chu an hung hnawl ṭan a. Tu cho ruol loa inngaia an insan puom leiin Indopui I & 2 an tuok a, hnèna an changa chu an inkhawngda dèr a, an ramper po po an chàn a, a tawpah thlierkar chikte bok kha an chang nawk ṭawk a nih. Sienkhom, lekhabu huoisen puong darna an ṭawng ruok chun ni tinin lien tieng a pan deu deu pei a nih. Khawvela ṭawng pakhat hmanga Baibul an inlet rawnna tak chu Sapṭawng (English) a ni a, English version hrang hrang 150 chuong a suok tah a nih.

Chuonga London a lo chau hnung chun lekhabu huoisen chun a hmunpui USA-ah a son dai a, chu taka chun hmunpui a la khuor zing a nih. Amiruokchu, mihaiin damtea an hnawl ruolin lekhabu huoisen khomin a thilthawtheina chu a hnawltuhai laka inthokin damten a kei kir ve a, an hnawl thlak pha leh a hnawl thlak ve el a nih. Chu ngirhmuna chun Amerika hi a ngir mek a, a hnawl hrat dan ruolin hnawlin a um hret hret pei a, a ropuina khom a kum têlin a tla hnuoi a, sienkhom khawvela sorkar hrat tak chu a la ni zing. Tuta an inṭhang anga an fe tung pei chun kum sawmhni velah China-in USA hnot phak a ta, India khomin kum sawmthum velah a hnot khèl ding niin mi thiemhai chun an ring. Chu pha chun lekhabu huoisen hin a hmunpui Delhi tlangah son naw nih ti thei a ni nawh.

Nang le keiin ei hriet ding le inchik tlak chu hi hi a nih: hi lekhabu huoisen hin a chawimawitu chu chawimawiin a dom sang a, a hnawltu chu a hnawl ve el a nih. Hi lekhabu huoisen, a um ti ei hriet ngai der lo chun kum zabi sawmpakuona hnukhawi tieng khan, ei chengna ram kilkhawr a hung tlung ve hlol el a. A hung hawntuhai chu Grik le Roman literechara inthoka varna lo inchuk tah, English pachal Savidge le Lorrain an nih. An lekhabu huoisen chu innghatnaa hmangin, A AW B mi siempekin, ziek hmanga ei literechar bul an mi hung ṭanpui a. Chuongchun, thu le hlaa inril Hebrai, Grik le Roman hai literechar zungpui chu ei lo zom ve tah a nih. Chu bul chu sirsana hmanga Thangngurin thu le hla a phu suokhai dam hi hang bi chieng inla, thingtlangpa thluok le ngaituonain a hawl phak lo rama tla, inril le inthuk, mawi le thlum, rangkachak le lunghlu tlang vong a nih. Chîk nawkzuola ei bi chun, ‘Hmar ṭawng’ ei ti tak bulṭhut ei rem ṭanna khom hi niin a’n lang bakah hi sula inthok hin ‘Hmar hnam’ ei ti khom hi a pieng niin a’n lang. Ei ngirhmun lo pal tahai lungnona ṭhang loa fîm taka ei bi pha leh hieng lai thlalak hi hmu fie tak tak thei chauh niin ka hriet.

Hi lekhabu huoisen hin lekhabu dang chu inngeipui a nei thei hrim hrim nawh. Hi lekhabu hi a nih, “Kei hi lampui le thutak le hringna chu ka nih; keimaa fe naw chu tu khom Pa kuom an tlung ngai nawh” (Joh 14:6) ti ngam ngat chu! Chuleiin, a hung tlung phata inthokin ei khawvela thu le hla lo ṭo le inṭhanglien tah hai chu a’n ngeipui naw nghal a, suotthlak tumin ṭhang a khaw nghal a nih. Prof. Siamkima hril dan bokin, lam lailung ngat naw chu a hraw nuom naw a, ei rama thil ṭo, thu le hla lo um ta sahai chun an ngam naw a, lampui inkiena lam sir inchuin an kat rok rok a, pung chu hril lo, ngai khom an aw hlei thei naw a, a thu hnuoiah inchêp tak le thawpik takin an um ta lem a nih. Chuong hun laia a rîl rem zawng taka ei kalchar le thu le hla zieranga inthuom a, sungpui chan chang dinga mawi taka lo chei thiemtu Thangngur le mi hrang hrang ei lo nei kha ei vangnei hle a nih.

Hi lekhabu huoisen hin a dong thiemtu taphot thu le hla le kalchar a ching châu hrep tah ze mawi chi hrang hrang tho chu an literechar thar cheimawinaa hmang nawkin, inphalamna nei dêr loin, tu mitmei khom vêng loin a chawi sang el a nih. Tu laia Western literature khom hi chuong ang vong chu a nih. Hi lekhabu an tiem tam nawna ram taphot chu literechar tieng an inhnuoi ti inla, ei hril suol ka ring nawh. Buddhism le Muslim sakhuo biekna ramah literechar ropui a suok der nawh. India ram thlir inla, Mahabharata le Ramayana hai hin literachar sukhausa zawngin sin an thaw nawh. Tu laia India ramin literechar-a hma a hung sawnna khom hi Grik le Roman literechar lo inchuk tah, lekhabu huoisena inthoka varna lo dong tah English mihai kuoma inthok boka an rochung a nih. Rangoon-a ka um laiin Zo hnathlakhai laia mani hnam ṭawnga Baibul tiem le sung inkhawm nei hlakhai le chuong thil chu thaw ngai lo sungkuo ka khaikhin a, a hmasa hi hnam le sakhuoah an nghet a, a nuhnung pawl hi chu bo dawbola bo, an lungril le hoiher iengkim Kawl mizie put an ni tlangpui. Mani ṭawnga Baibul tiem hlak hi hnam damna dinga bulṭhut poimaw a lo ni takzet.

Chu chu a ni lai zingin ei bi chieng ngai thil a um. Chu lekhabu huoisen, Lusei ṭawnga inthoka eini ṭawnga ei inlet chu a ṭhen kum 1946 laia inthokin, a rêngin kum 1968-a inthokin ei hmang ṭan ve a, nasa takin ei ṭangkaipui a, taksa le thlarauah malsawmna ei dong rawn hle. Amiruokchu, hla tienga bulṭhut indik mi ṭanpuitu Thangngur le a zawlpuihai hniekhnung zuia hma ei sawn pei laiin, thu tieng hma ei sawn hrat thei dèr nawna san le ei ṭawng ziek ei thiem naw deu deu amanih ti dinga a um el hi a san bi chieng a ngaiin ka hriet. Kum tam ka sui hnunga a san bul, a kaikuongpa nia ka hmu chu thil dang ni loin Baibul inletnaa ṭawng indik lo ei hmangin a mi ṭhuoi suol lei a ni tak. Ei lo hmang suol sawt tah leiin a mi ṭhuoi suol a nih ti ngaituo nachang khom ei hriet ta naw a, a suolna lai lai kha thlarau tuihang pai rawnah ei ngai el tah khom a hoi. Chu chun ei Pathien thu thlirna mit khom hi a tlawm takah 45 dikri velin a sukkal niin a’n lang bok. Hi mit inkalna hi siem ṭhat ding chun kum tam ngai a tih. Chu thil chu invoiin Delhi Version Baibul hin hma a lak ṭan a, lekhabu huoisen tak a nina zie le rong put ve phak, ṭawng hmang dan indik ei hung inchuk theina lekhabu a ni ngei beiseia buotsai a nih.

(Delhi, April 26, 2008)
