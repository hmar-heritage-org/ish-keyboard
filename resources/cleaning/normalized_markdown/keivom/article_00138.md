Thla hni lai liem tah khan South India-ah kar khat (Sept 21-27), chu zoah USA-ah kar hnih (Oct 18- Nov 4) inzin dingin prokram ka siem a, tiket khom ka sukfel vong tah a. Chennai ka pan tawmin Hmar Inpui President Dr. John Pulamte-in a mi hung telefawn a, Shillong tieng Hmar ṭawng thua inpawl khawmna fienriel an buotsai mek thu le chu taka Resource Person pakhat-a an mi ruot thu a mi hung hril a. Kei chun, “To ta, a ni dinga in ruot October 22-24 hi New York-a um dinga prokram ka sukfel sa a na, ka va hung ṭhang thei hmel naw de! A hmain lo hre lawk met inla chu ni ding maw!” ka ta. Ama chun, “A va zie naw de! Nang a meizàng hlaptu tak dinga ei beisei i ni a, hung ṭhang hrâm dan lampui hung dap ta la” a ta. A hnungin David Buhril leh voi hnih voi thum kan hril tlang hnungin, ka USA inzin ding chu son hlain, Seminar-a ṭhang ngei dingin thutlukna ka siem fel tah a nih.

Fienrielna hmasa tak

Ka hriet ve phak china chuh, tuta ṭawng thua finrielna buotsai mek hi a hnam pum huopa National-level seminar nei hmasakna tak ding a nih. Tu kum July 18-19, 2013 khan Aizawlah 2-DAY NATIONAL SEMINAR ON MIZO LANGUAGE: CONTEMPORARY CHALLENGES & PROSPECTS ti an huoihot a, Resource Person dinga an mi fiel leiin Delhi-a inthokin ka hang ṭhang ve a, chai ding thupui 20 an thlang suoka, ka hlawkpui hle. Anni khoma State level-a Mizo ṭawng thua national seminar an nei hmasa tak a nih. Chai ding thu poimaw 20 an thlang suoka inthok hin ṭawng chungchanga hril tlang ding hang tamzie a sukchieng hlein kei chun ka hriet. Chuong sà khom chun 25% vel chauh kan tawk hman niin ka hriet. Ka topic dinga an ruot chu, Mizo language as a Mizo link language ti a ni a, a paper tak chu Sapṭawngin ka ziek a, ka tiem tak ruok chu Mizo ṭawngin ka ziek thung a. A pahnia inkopin thu mal 6800-a sei a nia chu a tlangpui ringot ka lawr phak den den chauh a nih. Ka sabzek ringot khom hi ni hni sunga chai zo seng chi a ni nawh. Literechar, thu le hla tieng lem chu a ke hmawr khom kan hlim hman naw hrim hrim.

Eini rawi, ei awm suok thu le hla ṭha hril ding mumal la nei lo, a inpui nghaktua inngai ṭhenkhat hlak chu A AW B tiem dan le hmang dana la buoi phak chauh nia inlang, ṭawng ziekna hawrop le literechar danglamna khom la hre hrang lo ni hiel awma la ṭawng invai hlak hai dam lem chun ei hma tawn ding hang pikzie hi ei la hmu phak der naw khom a hoi khop el. Hnam hmangaina nei phak der lo ang ziezanga la khawsa, hieng anga hmasawnna lampui hril le dap dinga finriel rotna dam hi lawmpui neka dodal a, a hma ang boka Inpui khom sukhmasawntu hmangruo nêka khaptu kuta hmang nuom pawl ei la um a hoi a, a poi khop el. Inpuiin hmathlir sukzau a, a theina kong iengkimah hma la a, lampui indika ram le hnam ṭhuoi dinga kal hung pên a, hnam pum huopa ṭawng chungchanga fienrielna a buotsai hi lawm a um takzet a, a hlawtling ngei khom ei ditpui a nih.

Ṭawng rurel

A bila tho lo ti ang elin, eini rawiin ei ṭawng hung suok dan le a hung inṭhang pei dan hi inbul deua ngaina ei nei leiin, sukhmasawn dinga ṭhang lak nekin a humhal zawnga ṭhang lak ei tum a, humhal ding tak tak ei nei si naw a, ei buoi a nih. Chuleiin, ei hril tlang ding hmasa tak chu ei ṭawng rurel hung insiem dan, Structure of Hmar Language a nih. A rurel le zierang ei hriet chieng phá phà leh a hmang dan le ziek dan grammar chu Sapṭawng grammar thlek ni loin ei ṭawng thlek danin fepui ei ta, hung infuk ṭan a tih. Tuta china ei thaw tak chu Sapṭawng grammar durbina inthoka thlir a, an grammar anga ei grammar duong ei tum a, sienkhom diametrically opposite language a ni leiin ei buoi a nih.

Ei buoina dang pakhat chu ziek zom le ziek zom naw thilah a nih. Ieng leia ei buoi? A san a um a, chu chu ei hriet fie hmasak phot a ngai. Ei ṭawng hi Chinse ṭawnga inthoka hung peng dar pei laia ṭawng pakhat niin ei hril. Chu ṭawng chun zierang poimaw pahnih a nei a. Pakhat chu thumal tin umzie le kawk nei, mono-syllabic language a nina hi a nih. Mal tin umze nei ṭawng a ni leiin ziek kop lo vong chu ziek dan ding tlangpui hmasa tak chu a nih. Amiruokchu, thumal pahni pathum kei kopa thumal hlawm khat, umzie le kawk nei (polysyllabic) bik ei siem pha leh chuong thu malhai chu umze hrang seng nei ni hai sien khom ziek kop ding a nih. Entirnan, sa (animal), sakei (tiger), sakkeibaknei (lion. ‘Sa’ hi a root word a ni a, hi le kei kopa thu mal hlawm hung suok za tam tak a um: safie, sakhi, sazuk, savom, saha, saruol, sanghal, sazaw, saphivok, sasen, saza, sabengtung, sakor, saku, saphu, saphai, sanak le a dang dang.

Zierang pahnina chu thumal ziek dan inang char, sienkhom ei lam rik dan le ei thu hmang kop zira umzie hrang daih nei, phonetic or tonal language a nina chu a nih. Ri dang dang put, a ṭhen chu lam hni la thuma inri hrang, a ṭhen ruok chu lam ruk chena ri hrang nei a ni a, chuonghai chu risâng (thluksei), rilaihawl (thluksei), rilaihawl (thluktawi), rihnuoi (thluksei), rihnuoi (thluktawi) le rilawn dam a ni a, thlûk sinsiena (diacritic mark) sie a ngai. Tuta china sinsiena ei hmang chu ‘v’ letling ‘^’ chauh a ni a, kawk a nei tlawm.

Chun, ei ṭawnga thil khikhawk deu pakhat chu thumal thuhmun char ni si, a kawk khom inang char, amiruokchu a hmaa thumal ei lamrik dan dungzuia lam dan danglama lam ngai a um a. Entirnan, Thanga lawmman dong ti le Hlîra lawmman dong tia ‘lawmman’ hmasaa ‘lawm’ ti hi rilaihawl thlukseiin feng a ta, lawmman pahnina hi risang thlukseiin fe thung a tih. A san iem ning a ta? A hmaa thumal lam rik khan a hnunga ri ding kha a thunun amanih hĕm (control, command) lei a nih. ‘Lawma lawmman dong ding chu a ṭha” ti-a hin ‘Lawma’ hi thluksei risanga ka lam chun, a zuitu ‘lawm’ khom hi chu ang ri chun a fe a; ‘Lawma’ hi thluksei rilaihawla ka lam chun a zuitu ‘lawm’ khom hi chu ri ang chun feng a tih.

Hmar: Old Kuki Group ṭawng tuolleng

‘Hmar’ ti ei hriet le pom dan infuk lo leia Hmar ṭawng ṭobul le hung inṭhang dan ni dinga ei ngai hin ei pawlitiks le ei ṭawng ei fepui dan a ṭhuoi leiin, Lusei ṭawng hmangtuhaiin ‘Mizo’ ti umzie an pom dan angin thil a fe a, ei unau danghai, Old Kuki ti hminga an lo kohai kha Hmar pahnam ang mei meia pom tlat tumna a um a, chu chun kâr nasa tak a siem. Chuong ang bok chun ei ṭawng hmang khom hi ni dingin ei ngai bik. Lusei ṭawng chu link language a hung ni angin, Hmar hnathlakhai ṭawng le Lusei ṭawng inchekpola ṭawng thar hung suok, ‘Khawsak ṭawng’ an ti hlak, a hnunga ‘Hmar ṭawng’ tia hung inṭhang suok hi Old Kuki laia ṭawng naupang tak khom ni sien link language angin a hung um ta a, chu chu ni khat thil thua um thut a ni naw a, san hrang hrang a nei. Chuleiin, Hmar as Old Kuki Link language ti hi thupui taka ei nei a ngai. Chu hrietthiemna chun pawlitiks le ei ṭawng inṭhang pei dan ding khom nasa takin ṭhuoi a ta, Hmar hnathlak hmun hrang hranga chenghai lai MIL sabzek la hung pung deu deu bok an tih.

Hawrop siem phuisui sin

Thla thum an um hman chauh tia misawnarihai hawrop mi siempek, fel hlel deu el ei hmang song tum hi ngaituona hmangtu ta ding chun pom zam el thei chi a ni nawh. Bulṭanna (elementary tool) dinga an mi duongpek kha a ṭha takpa sawna humhal tum tlat pawl ei um hi ei ṭhangmawbawk pakhat a nih. Siemphuisui pei a mamaw a, nuom le nuom naw thu khom a ni nawh. Hawrop inkop AW, CH le NG an lo siem hi bàn a, English/Roman alphabet po po ei ṭawng thluk mila her le lama hmang ding a nih. Chuleiin, ‘Hawrop siem phuisui’ ti hi thupuia hmanga beipui thlak a, chai tak tak a hun tah. Hi thupui hnuoia hin AW le O le TR hmang dan chauh khom ni lo, J & Z hmang dan dik, hawrop ṭhieng (AW,CH, NG) nei a ṭul le ṭul naw etc po po chai ding a nih. Chun, thluk sinsiena (diacritic marks) khom hril sa lo thei lo hung ni bok a tih. Hi sabzek ringot khom hi kâr pumhlum khoma chai zo seng lo thil a nih.

Ṭawng hmang indik

Ei hriet naw lei amanih fimkhur naw leia ṭawngkam a kawk naw zawnga hmang dam, ziek inzom naw ding thu dam le thil dang, common errors suina hi thupuia hmanga ei ngirhmun phor lang hi thil ṭûl makmaw a nih. Chuong thil chu hril seng lo, indik thlup anga an mi hmangpui, entirnan, “Siloam dilah va sawp rawh” tia ṭawng thiem naw anga Isu an inṭawngtir dam hi, a tam. Hmai ei phi a, ei sawp ngai nawh. Ei ṭawng thatzawl chu pulpit a nizie le ei Baibula ṭawng indik lo ei hlu lut po po ṭawngsie chu ei phur a nih ti dam chen hung kang kai a tih. Chu le inzoma ṭawng hmang indik kohranin a ngai poimaw pei theina ding, Mizoram Synod-in a’n khat tawka an rongbawltuhai ṭening an pek anga eini khomin ei thaw a ṭul takzie chenin ei hril phak lem chun, a taka zuk hmang el thei thil tam tak ei hawn ka ring.

Regulatory Body ei mamaw am?

Ram ṭhenkhata chu ṭawng sukhmasawnna dinga hma nasa taka la a, diksawnari puithu tak tak siemtu dingin Language Regulatory Body sorkarin a nei a, an sor ṭangkai hle a; ram ṭhenkhata ruok chu sor ṭangkai nekin konkaw phanain an hmang bok. Ṭawng hin ni tin hma a sawn a, a’n thlak tung pei a, ṭawng hlui a ral a, a tharin a hung thlak a. Khawsak dan le nun inthlak thleng leia hmang zing ta lo ṭawngkam tam tak chu humhal a, umze thar inputtirin ṭangkai takin an hmang zui pei a. Chuong ang thawnaa hmangraw poimawhai chu chanchinbu hai, ziek mi hai, thil thar siemtu hai le a dang dang, an nih. Chuonghai chu zalen taka an sin inthawtir neka daltu-ah Regulatory Body hi a ṭhang hlauh thei bok a. Chuleiin, eini khomin Regulatory Body ei mamaw am? ti hi nasa taka chai a hun tah. Hi thupui hnuoia hin Manipur-a Hmar Literature Society (HLS) in Regulatory Body anga insie a tum hi ram le hnam ta dinga thil ṭha a ni le ni naw bakah State danga HLS le an inlaichin dan ding khom hril tlang thei ning a tih.

Translation

Hieng ei hril tah hai naw khom hi ṭawng sukhmasawnna le humhalna dinga hmangruo poimaw- role of media, church, educational institutions, public and private organisations and offices, art & culture, reference books, particularly dictionaries etc hril ding a tam. Chu chun literechar huonga Translation poimawzie khom ei la hril phak nawh. Chutaka chun ei lekhabu tiem rawn tak Baibul khom hung inlang phak a ta, chu thu ringot khom chu kar tam buon tling a nih. Khawvela tu hnam el khom hi thu le hlaa hausa saa pieng an um naw a, dam tea an ngaituona le khawsakna hung changkang pei ruola an ṭawng le an thu le hla khom hung inṭhang tung pei a nih.

Tu hnam khomin mani intodelna tawk thu le hla an nei hma chun, an kawl le kienga hmasawn lem le changkang lemhai thu le hla hawa khawsa an nih. Thu le hla ṭha, anni ta dinga ṭangkai dinga an hriethai chu anni ṭawngin an inlet a, chu chun an ṭawng nasa takin a sukhausa pha hlak. Entirna ṭha tak nia ka hriet chu Hebrai le Grik ṭawnga inthoka ṭawng hrang hranga Baibul an inlet hi a nih. Chu chu tu laia literechar khawvela hnam hausa taka ngai English hai khoma bulṭanna taka an hmang a nih. Mizorama khom matriculation-a Lushai Vernacular an hung nei ṭan khan Kristian Van ram Kawng Zawh (Pilgrim’s Progress) kha textbook-ah an hmang a, keini huna chen khom khan kan la hmang a nih. College level-a MIL an hung nei khan Joba lekhabu le Baibula bu dang dang thlang suok textbook-ah an hmang bok. J.F Laldailova letling Shakespeare drama khom an hmang nasa bok.

Kum 1973 khan College level-a hmang ding tiin Rabindranath Tagore-in nobel prize a hmuna Gitanjali chu Hmar ṭawng kan let a, kum 40 hnungah tu hin College level-ah MIL ei lo nei tah leiin a bua sut dingin buotsai mek a nih. Kum 1962-a Shakespeare drama Julius Caesar kan let ruok kha chu ka hmu zo ta nawh. Translation hi sin hautak a ni a, a ang charin a suok thei naw a, a ṭha lem amanih a ṭha naw lemin a suok ngei ngei. Thaw fuka ei inngai chang chu vanzawlah ei inleng a, a fuk thei der naw chang chu Sheol-a li lut angin thaw a pik a, thuok a’n sam el a nih.

Literechar bukna lung hmanga bel chieng dawl Hmar ṭawnga translation works ka la hmu naw a, a um a ni khomin a tam ka ring nawh. Ei hmang tak chu BSI sut Baibul hi a ni a, hieng anga Hmar ṭawng suosamna lekhabu hi ka la hmu ngai nawh. Tu kum 2013-a suok, BSI sut bok, Muolhoi Presbyterian-hai sum bituma thaw kha a ṭawngkam thuom hlui inthla el naw sien khom a thu a hung fie ta a, lawm a um takzet a nih. Hmar ṭawng hi a dam khawsuok ding chun Baibul inlet ṭhat lo thei lo nia kan hriet leiin Delhi Version (2007) hi kan buotsai a nih. Hieng thila dam hin mani ṭawng le Pathien ram hmangaituhai chun siem phuisui pei dan dapa inrawn khawma seminar le worksho nei chur chur awm ei ni vei leh, ei thaw naw chauh khom ni loin, chuong ang thaw rottu ṭhahnemngaina chu dema kal pawl ei um hlak.

Chu indaidanna bang tirdakum tak chu Shillong seminar-cum-retreat hin ṭhiekin hung sukchim sien, hmai intuoa unau inbein, kar nei loin hma ei tawn pei dan ding hril tlangna dawkan hung ni sien nuom a um takzet. Ram le hnam hmasawnna dinga kalchawina hi a umzie hre nuom lo, rûlin ‘ka mei sir sien, chuk hrep lang’ a ti anga lo insèru pawl le ha tât ngei pawl an um palh a ni khomin, hnam dam khawsuokna ding zongtu Inpui chun saipui lam lawn thaw a, lam sira lo inbauhtu uicho ieng zat um hai sien khom, ṭhahnemngainaa sip, Kuokluong lal, fam tah, Lalkhum Keivom zai zawmin, HMA TIENG KE PEN EI TU….AW PATHIEN, KAN RAM KAN PEK CHE ti rop zingin.

(October 5, 2013, Delhi)
