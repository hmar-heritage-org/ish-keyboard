LAWMTHU HRILNA

Aizawl – 28/11/2023

Tuilêt hnunga la um ngailo, a vawikhatna a dingin HSA Office Aizawl ah a um ta ding.

HSA JHQ Aizawl ṭhuoituhaiin hun iemani chena inthawka an lo buoipui le an lo nawr, HSA in Aizawl ah Office nei ve ta inla ei ti chu Pu Lalthlamuana Hmar, CEM, Sinlung Hills Council in hma a mi lâk pêka ama zârin HSA in Aizawl khawpui ah ei Office ei ti ve thei ding, ei hang nei thei ta dingin a um a, lawmum ei ti takzet.

Pu Lalthlamuana Hmar, CEM SHC chungah HSA chun lawmthu a hril tak meu meu a, hienga ram le hnam ngaituo, Hmar nauhaiin hnam dang hai ei inhnar bîk zing en thei lova tak ngeia hma mi la pêktu CEM ei nei hi HSA chu a lâwm takzet a nih. A damkhuo hrui ang sei sien, Pathienin malsawmin ṭhuoi pei rawsen, ti hi ei ṭawngṭaina a nih.

Vawisun khan a hmun ding hai President rawiin JE hai leh zu in fel a nih.
