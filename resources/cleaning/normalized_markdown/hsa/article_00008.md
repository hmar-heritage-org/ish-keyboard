DEMNA THUPUONG

19th March, 2019

Ni 17 March, 2019 (Pathenni), zâna Rengkai khawsûng a chêngna in silaia kâp a, ralthuom chelek a, nu

le nau suk thlabar zawnga che misuol hai hi keini Hmar inchuklai pawl (Hmar Students’ Association)

HSA chun thiemnaw inchangtirin a dem takzet a nih.

Ei hnam sunga inthithaina le ralthuom chelek zawng a chetna hrim hrim hi HSA chun iengtik lai khawm a

ditnaw a, dem zing a tih. Khawlai hmun a khawm nisien tu pawl khawm nisien mani hnam le khuo sunga

ralthuom chelek a hmalakna thil hrim hrim hi HSA chun a ditnaw hle a, khawlai hmun a khawm hieng ang

thil ditumlo hi intlungtir nawk ta ngainaw sien ti tu el khawm a’n hriettir bawk a nih.

Hieng ang pawisa nawtu tu pawl khawm nisien a thawtu le intlungtirtu pawl/mimal khawm nisien HSA

chun dem zing ata, a lo thawtu hai chu hnam inzanawtu le hnam ienga ngai lo le pawisa nawtuah ngai a

tih.

Hmar hnam dam zing rawse.

Josef V Tuolor

General Secretary,

HSA GHQ
