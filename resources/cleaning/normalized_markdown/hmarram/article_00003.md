Hmar Inpui Constitution 2026 chun Hmar hai nina(identity) chieng takin hieng hin a lo siem fel tah.

Keini, Sinlung suok Hmar nauhai hin lungruol le lungtlûkna det tak neiin, khawvel hmun tin, ramtina chêng Hmar nauhai kawng tinrênga hmasawn le inṭhang lien pei ding le Hmar ṭawng le Hmar hnam nunphung ṭha le mawihai hum le chawisâng ding le Zohnathlakhai kei bum le insuikhawmna dinga Mizo hnam kûngpui kan nina det taka chel tlata chung Pathien ditdana kalchawia inrêlbawl dingin, Thlaphal ni sawmthum, kum sang hni le pahni, Hmar Inpui Assembly hmasatak a hin hi Danthlungpui hi pawmin kan namdet ti kan puong.
