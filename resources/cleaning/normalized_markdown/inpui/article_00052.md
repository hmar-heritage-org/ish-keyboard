Hmar Inpui General Headquarters Executive Meeting a tumlina chu March 29, 2010 ni khan nei a ni a, an thu relhai chu hieng a hnuoia hai hi an nih.

1. NETV a Hmar Programme mumal taka nei dingin hma hrat taka lak mek a ni a; Pu L.B.Sinata thangin NETV intivithmupui nawk vat tum a nih.

2. Assam University a Hmar tawng undergraduate Level chena upgrade thawna dingin Rs. 5,000 pek a nih.

3. HYA General Headquarters in Office bawlna dinga ei ni laia Fund Raising thaw a tum chu lawmum a ti a, mi tlawmngaihai lai thlawlawm dawl an tum chu H.I. chun a phal pek.

4. TUMTUS (Tuiruong Vadung Multipurpose Tribal Upliftment Society) sung buoina chungchangah Hmar Inpui palaihai hmalakna ngaithlak a nih a, Hmar Inpui thutlukna an pawm thei ve ve ding a ni leiin Executive in lo bawzui peitu dingin mi pawimaw a ruot pek.

5. Hmar Inpui Barak Valley Region in General Hqrts. Hmar Inpuia Saisel Jhumland thubuoi rel pek dinga a hung intlun hi ngun taka Hmar Inpui Gen. Hqrts Executive Committee in a ngaituo hnungin hi ram hi ram le hnam ta dinga hmun pawimawtak a lo nizie hrietchieng a ni leiin Hmar Inpui Barak Valley Region thuoituhai ngenna hung siem angin a hmun ngeiah fe a, sukfel dingin thutlukna siem a nih. Chu hma po chu tuta Headman ni laizing Pu Zahmuok Khawzawl hi Headman ni zing a tih. Tukhawma a Headman nina inchupui lo dingin Hmar Inpui Gen. Hqrts Executive Committee chun a rel.
