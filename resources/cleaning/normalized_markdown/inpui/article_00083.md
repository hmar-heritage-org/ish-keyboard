By Joseph Joute, Freelance Journalist

Chanchin Tha na na na chu lawm naw ruol an kuo naw a ni hi! “Mitin ta dinga chanchin tha lawmum bek bek” kha a ni tlata, mitinin lawm a awm reng a nih. Hienglai zing hin hi Chanchin Tha kher hi chu lawm dan chi hni/kawng hni um theiin an lang. Ka tina san chu: khawvelin a hriet a, chatuona Hringna Thu chu mihriemna dana ringawt khawm, taksa thil taka lawm thei a ni a. Mi po po (mankind) sandamna dinga hung chu lawm naw ruol an naw reng a.

Chun, thlarau dan taka Pathienin a hmangaihai kuoma a pek le an hrietirna leia sung tienga inthawka lawmna chi khat a um bawk a. Ka hril umzie chu; Chanchin Tha hi taksa thil taka (lang-a-changa) lawmna le thlarau tieng taka, lungril le thlarau nun po poin a lawmna, taksaa inlang suok khawm a um thei ve ve ka tina a nih.

Chu entirna or fiena pakhat chu; Isu hung pieng laiia lungril tak le thlarau a lawm em em, nei po po maksan ngam khawpa hmu dinga fe hai, Berampuhai le thilpek tha tak tak le lawmna inentir, khawvel lalhai taksa dan taka inlawmpuinaa lawmpui khawsak mivar hai a inthawk khan a tira inthawkin a lo um tah sa a nih.

Ei lawm dan ding hi ieng tak him ni’ng a ta? ti hi mani senga ei inenfiena ding nisen. A that ve ve zing laiin, vawisuna a kawng hnia lawm theituhai chu mi vangnei tak an nih.

Chuonga mi vangnei chu Hmar hai ei nih ka ti tah a nih! Asan chu, thenkhat lungril le thlarau po poa lawm, taksa thil taka khawsak mivarhai anga lawm thei el lo, kum 10 lai zet lo inringlawka lawm thei chau ei um laiin, chawp-le-chila ropui taka lawm thei khawm ei um bawk a. Pathien malsawmna vawng chu a nih.

INPUMKHATNA AWNIN: Kum 2010 kumpui lingletin kohran hran hran, hnam hran hran haiin Gospel Centenary ei lawm sup sup tah a, a ropui a, Pathienin ei ta dingin thil ropui tak a mi thawpekhai ei lawm hle a. Ei lawm tawp nawh a, mani kohran chau dittawk ta lova hnam pumpui kohran po po huopa lawm nuomna chen a la hung ra suok pei a. Chu chu a nih chanchin tha thilthawtheina chu; piengzie, lungril, ngaidan, etc.inanglo vawng, pumkhata mi siem theitu “Chanchin” (Thu) a nina chu!

Mihriem dan taka, thiemna le varna dang dang le lampuihai hmanga impumkhat ei tum khawma thei ta lohai khawm, chanchin tha bawkin lungril hmunkhat putin a mi siem nawk a nih. Chu fiena (evidence) chu Dec.9-12 sunga Saikawta nei ding HMAR GOSPEL CENTENARY CELEBRATION ruohmana um mek hi a nih.

THE CELEBRATION OF THE CENTENARY: Tuta kum zabi (century) sunga ei rama dinga lawmmna la ropuitak ding chu Hmar Gospel Centenary Celebration 2010, Hmar Christian Leaders’ Forum (HCLF) in a buotsai mek hi la ning a tih. HCLF thuoina hnuoia kohran tin thang tlanga buotsai hi; 10,000 Choir buotsai a ni dan dam, program chi hran hran khawm duonglawk a ni zie kha hma khan ka lo hriet tah a. Amiruokchu, official taka confirm na a la um nawh a.

Nov. 14, Pathienni zingkar khan Pu Buonga (of LIA), Information & Public Relation tienga mawphurtu chu a hung leng a, HGCC program print vawng tah hi a mi hung pek a, a thei chun preview lo thaw dingin a mi hung ngen a. Program Booklet ka hang ena, thu ramtin kan hriltlang laia ka lungrila hung lut chu; hnam damna dinga chanchin tha mi hung petu ditdan ang takin Inpumkhatna kawng chu ei hraw mek a hawi a, hi Centenary Celebration hin ram le hnam, kohran inpumkhatna tak tak mi siempek thei a tim aw, ti hi a nih.

A program sung details hang en chun a pui het het hle a, Hmar kohran sunga hotu le thuoitu popo a fun khawm a na. Inkhawmpui danghai le an ang nawna dang pakhat chu Seminar & Discussion a um hi a ni a. A program preview ti lema, a dawt dawta thaw ding item hai zieklanga comment ka tum nawha. Publicity Dept. ditdan chie le thaw dan ding an ti khawm ka hriet chuong nawha, “Tha I ti dan danin,” an ti tho leiin, “as a whole” a enin comment inla ka ti ta lem a nih.

Dec. 9, 2010 zan dar 5:30 pm a tan ding program-a hin Centenary Celebration ei lo hmang tahai, Sielmat-a tanin, program kalhmang; Hall Inauguration, Dedication, Gospel Torch Lighting Ceremony, etc. hai a ni tlangpui vawng a. A thawtu dinghai chu tuipui Ral mi sap hai ni ta lovin ei rama kohran thuoituhai ngei an ni ta lem ti hi a nel a. Program contents hang ena, kohran hran hran thuoituhai hmun khata an hang participate na hin a mi them lem a, Rev. Dr. J.L. Songate, Rev. Dr. J. Huoplien Neitham, Rev. Lalneikhup Hmar, Rev. Ros Infimate, Rev. Dr. Lalhmuoklien, Rev. D. Ruolngul, Rev. Lungawiruol Khawbung, phek hmasataka an hming a hei um tlar duot hin thu tamtak a hril lemin ka hriet a.

“Pathien Thlarau Thienghlim chu Inremna Thlarau a nih” ti hi a fie zuolin an lang a. Tirko Paulan, “Inremna rawngbawl sin a mi pek a (2 Kor.5:18)a ti ang khan, chu rawngbawlna sin chu a tak taka rawngbawltu han an thawna sin niin ka hmu leiin ka lawmna a zuol a nih. Ka mimal taka chu, ei rawngbawltuhai hin ka lawmna an sukfamkim a nih. Pathien mi lawmna khawm famkim ngei sien nuom a um.

Hril talai lai, meeting hi Rev. Lalneikhup Hmar in keithuoi a ta, Rev. Dr. J.L. Songate in Hall hawng a ta. Rev. Jacob Pudaite in inhlan a ta, Rev. Dr. J. Huoplien Hmar in Gospel Torch sitkai a ta, Rev. Ros Infimate in Welcome Address hril a ta, Rev. Dr. Lalhmuoklien in Introduction thawng a ta, Rev. D. Ruolngulin thu hril a tih!

Kum 100 sungin ei ramah hieng neka inthuruolna le inremna neia Pathien biek tlangna (Worship Service) hi a la um ngai nawha, Gospel Centenary lawmna a ni leiin, CELEBRATION of the CENTENARY ka ti el a nih!

Program suk phuisuitu ding pakhat chu; 10,000 Voices Choir, tuta district tina an lo inchuk dup dup mek hi a nih. “Halleluia, Amen,” tia Sielmat a 1,000 voices han an hang rem dup dup hman kha a ropui a na, 10,000 thalai ngawtin Lalpa inpakna hla an hang rem lem chu ropui hleng a tih.

Inkhawm zata hla thuoina hi Music Department inrawi ding a ni bawka, Thlarauin mal a sawm bawk lem chun hla ringawt khawm hlawkpuina khawp tling ngei a tih.

Ni hmasatak Dec.10 zing dar 10 am hin Service um nawk a ta, hi hma hin Centenary Souvenir, ei nghakhla em em kha, Rev. Lalthankhum Sinate-in tlangzar a ta, Centenary dar vuokna le thil dang danghai um a ta, Gospel Message ruok chu um naw nih.

Sun dar 12:30 a inthawkin Seminar a ruol in hmun thum-in Topic pathum nei ning a ta. Men’s Group ah “Kohran le Inpumkhatna” ti ning a ta, Rev. Dr. J.L. Songate-in facilitate a ta, Moderator Rev. Lalrosiem Songate thang a tih.

A dawtah Seminar & Workshop on “Nuhmei Le Hmasawnna” nei ning a ta, Pi Lalthiengzo in facilitate a ta, Pi Ramsiemzo Moderator in thang a tih.

A sawng thumna (pathumna) chu Youth’s Group ta ding ning a ta, Topic: “Thalai le Inchukna” ti niin Facilitator Rev. Rohminglien Pakhuongte le Moderator ah Rev. Edwin Darsanglur thang a tih.

Hi ni zana hin Worship Service um a ta, Rev. Rothanglien Hmar in Pathien thucha hril a tih.

Dec. 11 dar 1:30 pm Seminar and Discussion um nawk a ta, Topic “2010 Khel Tienga Kohran Hai Kalchawi Dan Ding” ti a na, Rev. Ros Infimate Moderator ning a ta, Upa Dr. Lalsiemlien Pulamte facilitator in thang a tih. Secretary haia inthawkin Seminar Report ngaithlak ning a ta, “HMAR GOSPEL CENTENARY CELEBRATION DECLARATION 2010” chu tentative-in approval thaw ning a ta. (Hi CENTENARY DECLARATION hi a tawp zan leh Affirmation thaw ning a tih.)

Program danghaia Thuhriltu dinghai chu, ei rama international speaker suongum tak takhai, Rev. H. Chungthang Thiek, Rev. Dr. H.M. Songate, Pi Lal Rem Siem hai le Rev. Hrilrokhum Thiek hai ning an tih.

Hi celebration program hi Nuoi tel Budget siema buotsai a ni a, inkhawm sung hin HGCC Fund dawlna tum hni nei ni bawk a tih.

COMMENT: A Program fe danhai chu a tluong a, a pui em em bawk a. Tuhin Department tin khawm an inpeifel ang a ni tah a. Program booklet khawm sut zo vawng a ni tah a. Amiruokchu, program leaflet ka hmua ka lungrila hung lut pakhat chu; a publish-tu le print le design tu hming a chuong naw tlat a. Ka hriet thoa chu mipui tam lem chun hrie thei naw ni hai. A suk official naw metin ka hmu a.

Chun, Centenary logo kha chitein phek hmatieng sie a na, Logo a thu ziek hai an tiem thei nawh. Chun, a phek (cover) outside ve ve ah Scenery (nal tak)sie a nia, a nala chu umzie a nei ka ti nawh. Ei tlangram khawm ni chie lo, ei khawpui khawm ni chie lo, kha nek khan Senvon lim dam a back-ground ah um sienla, phek hnung tieng Churachandpur lim (picture map) dam um lem sienla, Senvon a inthawka kum 100 hnunga Tuithaphai (CCpur) tina ang deuin.

Chun, cover sung tieng ve ve blank (a ruoka) sie nekin HGCC Logo lien deuvin, a thuziekhai tiem thei vawng dingin sie ni lem sienla, phek hmataka Fielna a um angin phek hnung tak (inside cover ah) lawmthu Hrilna (note of appreciation) tawite um bawk sien. A sut mant tlawmte in a suksang met el theia chu, phuisui deu a ta, Hmar hnam pum huopa thilthaw ding chun puithu deu a ta, sukfamkim bawk a tih.

Program Booklet sira Hmar puon le Thangsuo puon tiel suk ruok hin chu hnam identity a suklanga, Pathien a chawimawi niin ka hriet.

HGCC hi Hmar hnam pumpuiin Pathien ei chawmawi theina hun remchang tak a ni leiin, Hmar-Kristien ka nih inti taphawt chu thangna chi ning a tih. Tuta tuma thang naw chu, naupang kum khat mi khawm, kum 100 dang hnunga um nawk chau ding a ni leiin, “Lenlai Chân” chu a nepte, “Damlai Chân” an ni ringawt ding a nih! Chatuon Hringna Thu khawm a um a nih! Amen!
