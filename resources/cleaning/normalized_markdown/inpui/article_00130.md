# IENG ZIERANG AM EI PUT DING?

* **Source URL:** [https://inpui.blogspot.com/2011/09/ieng-zierang-am-ei-put-ding.html](https://inpui.blogspot.com/2011/09/ieng-zierang-am-ei-put-ding.html)

By L.Keivom, Inpui Columnist

Babel hmun, ṭawngsemzawla Pathienin ṭawng a sukdanglam vong thu Genesis 11-a inziek kha changchawiin, mi tam tak chun ṭawng hi hnam tin kuoma Pathien pek niin an hril hlak. Khawvela thil um po po hi Pathien pek vong anga ngai chun, chu thlirna chu a’n dikna chin um hrim a tih. Amiruokchu, thil hung inher suok dan chiktuhai chun ṭawng hi Pathien pek ni loin, a hmangtu mihriemhai hung ser le ching suok a ni lemzie inhmai naw ni hai. Ṭawng hi Pathienin siem saa a mi pek ni loin mihriemin ei mamaw ang dungzui peia ei ser suok pei a ni lem.

Pathienin mihriem kuoma thu a hrilin tu hnam ṭawng khom a hmang ngai nawh. Mi a biek pha leh ama ṭawng a hmang a, chu chu a biekhai khan a umzie anni ṭawng angin an lo hriet a, chu chu anni ṭawngin an puong suok el a nih. Baibula Thuthlung Hlui huna Pathienin mihriem hmanga a thu hrilhai chu Hebrai ṭawngin an lo ziek thlak a. Thuthlung Thara mihriem kuoma Pathien inpuongna ruok chu Grik ṭawng deu vonga ziek a ni thung. Chu thil lei chun Pathienin Hebrai ṭawng amanih Grik ṭawng amanih a hmang tina a ni nawh. Rev. Thangngur kuoma Pathien inpuongna, hlaa a ziek suokhai chu Hmar ṭawngin a ziek suok leiin Pathienin Hmar ṭawng a hmang tina a ni naw a, Pathienin Hmar ṭawng hmangtu a hmang tina a ni lem.

Ṭawng hi mihriemin ei chengna khawvel, sik le sa, tlang le phai le a mi tuom veltu thil dang dang le inzoma ei ngaituona le suongtuonahai hril suokna hmangruo dinga ri hrang hrang ei hung ser suok le chîng suok a nih. Eini rawi ṭawng ṭobul, thu mal hung insiemna hnar ei bi chieng chun a hung inṭanna chu thu mal lam rik pakhat vong a nih. Thu mal pakhat, ziek dan inang chu ei lam rik dan zirin thil dang dang ei inkawktir a, umzie hrang hrang a hung nei a. Chuleiin, ei ṭawng hi ṭawng bul le umzie suituhai chun a ria innghat, thu mal tin kawk nei ṭawng, phonetic and mono-syllabic language an lo ti chu a nih. Chu chu Mongoloid hnamhai ṭawng fe dan tlangpui chu a ni vong.

Entirna dingin thu mal pakhat ‘mei’ ti hi hang la inla. A umzie bul chu mei (fire) le mei (tail) tina a ni tak a. Amiruokchu, thu mal dang pakhat pahnih belin ṭawngkam za chuong dai ser thei a nih. Chuong laia ṭhenkhat chu a tira mei umzie le inzomna nei ta der lo dam a nih. Ei lam non a, “A thaw mei mei (carelessly); Kan titi mei mei (leisurely); Mi ti mei mei naw rawh (disapproval); Bu fa mei mei ei tiu aw (simple, informal); A va lawmum mei mei de! (unexpected windfall); Mei mei a! (What nonsense! What rubbish! Don’t be stupid!); Mi mei mei an nawh (not a simpleton, not ordinary, an important person, a strong character) ei ti chun a tira a umzie ei hril leh inzomna an nei ta naw vong ti hre thei ei tih.

Chun, “Mei nei (mei+nei) deuin ṭawng naw rawh” ka ti chun a thu hrila khan thu inthup, sienkhom kawk amanih umzie nei rieu, (sinister meaning, insinuation, innuendo, ambiguity), hril dot si loa a hmawr ka phor lang thil a um tina a nih. Chun, ‘mei mit’ ti umzie chu mei that, sukhlum tina ni sienkhom a letlinga ka sie chun ‘mit mei’ a ni ta dai a, chu taka inthok chun ṭawngkam tam tak, umzie inang naw vong a hung suok thei. A mi mit mei (a lustful glance or disapproval look); Mit mei a mi’n hmu (approval look); Mit mei kawi a mi’n hmu (showing disapproval); A mit mei veng rawh (to take care not to offend) tihai hi umzie inang lo vong a nih. Hienghai hi ei ṭawng thu mal lam khata inthoka ṭawngkam hrang hrang an hung insiem dan a hmawr ei thlir thuok theina dinga ei hung tar lang a nih.

Ṭawng hi a hmangtuhai zirin ni tinin a thar an siem ang bokin ei hmang ta nawhai chu a thi pei a. Thu mal ei nei nawhai chu hnam tinin ei mamaw ang peia ei ser suok an naw leh ei kawl le kienga hnam chenghai ṭawng ei haw seng a, chuong ei ṭawngkam hawhai le ei ṭawng chu hung inthlop tungin ṭawng thar a hung insiem pei a. Chuong hnam dang ṭawng ei lakhai chu eini ṭawng thlek dan anga thle ve le zierang inang a ni chun a hrat lem lemin damten a hrat naw lem chu a lem hmang a, sienkhom lema um chun lem a ni ti khom a hriet ngai nawh. Chuong ang chu Sino-Tibeto-Burman group hnuoia ṭawng hrang hrang ei hmanghai hi a ni vong. A zungpui tak chu Chinese ṭawng a na, chu chu Tibetan ṭawngin a hung chekpol a, ei hung thlang tlak pei hnungin Burmese ṭawng leh a hung inchekpol nawk pei a, chu taka inthoka fe peiin India hmar sak tienga hnam hrang hrang ṭawng hi a hung pieng a nih.

Ei kawl le kienga hnam dang ṭawng ei lak lut hin ei ṭawng zierang a sukhausa thei ang bokin a sukbo der thei bok a. Ei ṭawng ṭobul le inzom ṭawng hrang hrang ei lak lut peihai chu ei ṭawng sukhausatu le a zierang sumrisang anga siem theitu an nih. Sienkhom, ei ṭawng zierang le inang der lo, entirnan Sapṭawng, ei lak lut rawn taluo chun sawtnawteah ei ṭawng a up hlum der thei a nih. A san chu ei ṭawng hung insiemna hnar le a fe dan kalhmang a danglam dai lei a nih. Sapṭawng thu mal hmangin eini ṭawngin ṭawngkam thar ser thei a ni nawh. Ei ṭawng zierang a put thei dan um sun chu ei ṭawng thluka ei thu mal lak luthai chu inthuomtir a nih.

Mani ṭawnga ei nei lo, hnam dang ṭawng lak lut hi hnam tin thaw dan a nih. Chuonga thaw nasa tak le huomhap tak chu Sapṭawng hmang hnamhai hi an na, an lak luthai chu anni rongin an inthuomtir vong a, chuong anga an inthuomtir hnung chun, an ta angin mi dang an min hmangtir hlak a nih. Chuong laia ram hming, Baibula chuong rawn em em anni awphawia an ko pakhat chu einiin ‘Aigupta’ ei ti, ‘Egypt’ an ti daih hi a nih. Tien lai chun, a ram mihaiin Aigupta hi ‘Kemet’ an ti hlak a, Nile chuonchung le chuonhnuoi pil hi sen ve lo, hàng hmuk el a ni leiin Kemet (pil hângna ram) an lo ti a nih. Kanan mihai chun ‘Misri’ an ti a, chu zul chun Arab-haiin ‘Misr’ an ti bok a, Hebrai mihai ruok chun ‘Misrayim’ an ti thung. B.C 700 vel, Grik mi thu le hla thiem Homer hun lai daia inthok tah khan Grikhai chun ‘Aiguptos’ an ti ve thung a, chu taka inthok chun eini khomin ‘Aigupta’ tiin ei mi hmasahaiin an mi lo siepek a, ei kam le khom an rem a, a fuk hlein ka hriet. Eini rawi lai hin a ieng a khaw ngaituo chik lo, Sapṭawng ngaizawng pawl ṭhenkhatin, ‘Egypt’ ti chu awrizinal lema ngai le ‘Aigupta’ ti chu tuolṭo bek bek, mamuli deua ngaia inzapui nuom dam an umin an lang a. Hi ngirhmun hi bi chieng a ṭul hlein ka hriet.

‘Zoram Khawvel’ ka ziek ṭan lai le tu chena mi thiem ṭhenkhata inthoka soiselna ka dong hlak pakhat chu Sapṭawng thu mal ei lak luthai hrim hrim chu, a thei phot chun, Zoṭawng awphawia ka ziek vong lei a nih. Chu chu Japan rama inthoka Amerika ram chena hnam tin thaw dan tlangpui a nih. Ei mi hmasahai lo thaw dan khom a ni leiin Sapṭawng le ṭawng danga mi thu mal ei lak luthai chun Zoṭawng zie a put tah leiin ei ṭawnga ṭhang hrim anga ngaina a um hiel tah. Chuong laia ei hmang inlar deuhai chu Borsap (Bara Saheb: Deputy Commissioner), Larsap (Lordship: Governor), Kudam (Godown), lalṭîn (khawnvar: lantern); hawldawl (puonthuo tuomna:hold-all); rizai (puonthuo:razai), sikul (school), ekzam (exam), rizal (result), talen (talent), sakramen (sacrament), tarpintel (turpentine), pulit (police) le a dang danghai an nih. Hieng thu mal

pulpit, pastor, angel, pen, pensil, sermon, sakramen, sikul

le a dang dang, ei ṭawng awphawia lam le ziekhai lem hi chu ei ṭawng ang charin ei inpuiah thaipui chan an chang tah.

Sapṭawng hre china ngaihai laia ngaidan nghet tak pakhat um chu: Sapṭawng thu mal ei hmanghai hi Sapṭawng ziek dan anga ziekin ei lam (sipel=spell) ding a nih, ti a nih. An ṭhangsan tak nia inlang chu, eini awphawia ei ziek pha leh tiem a sukharsa a, tiem suol a ol a, ei Sapṭawng lam dan khom nasa takin a ṭhuoi suol nuom, ti a nih. Hi ngaidan hi inza a um lai zingin ngaidan hmasiel le bui mei tawi ang a nina lai a um a; ei ṭawng vui liemna dinga lam ei sat ang a ni thei bok. Ngaidan hmasiel a nina chu, Sapṭawng hre lohai ngirhmun ding a dawn phak ve nawh. Bui mei tawi ang le chongkor mit puta thil thlir a nina chu, Sapṭawng awphawi le ziek dan anga ei thaw pei ding a ni chun, eini ṭawngah Sapṭawng hi a luong lut nasa si leiin, hun sawt nawteah ei ṭawng zierang thlak danglam vong a ta, a up hlum der ding a nih ti an ngaituo phak naw lei a nih. Sapṭawnga thu mal ei lak lut hi ei ṭawng sukhausakna dinga ei thaw a na, Sapṭawng sukhausakna dinga ei thaw a ni nawh ti hi hriet chieng a ṭha ngotin ka hriet. Chuong po po chunga poimaw chu, Sapṭawng root words ni loin eini root words hmang chauin ṭawngkam thar ser thei a nizie hi a nih.

Ka hmu dan chun, nuom inla, nuom naw inla, hma ei sawn ang dungzui peiin hnam dang ṭawng ei lak lut pei a ngai a, chuong ṭawnghai laia ei lak lut rawn tak ni pei dinga inlang chu Sapṭawng a nih. Chu chu ieng zierang am ei inputtir ding a na? Ei ṭawng zierang ei inputtir a, ei sumphukah, ei tapte bulah ei inṭumtir thiem chun, ei zierang putin eini lai inzel a ta, ei ṭawng sukhausatu ni bok a tih. Ama zierang ei inputtir ruok chun damten ei ṭawng hi ama zie le rongin thlak a ta, a tawpah ei ṭawng hi a up hlum ngei ngei ding a nih.
