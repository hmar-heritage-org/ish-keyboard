Hi lei hin hi Seminar nei ding chungchanga ka ngaidan ziek ve tha in ka hrieta ka hung ziek ve a nih. Hi seminar hi nei tha ka ti na a sawt ta a. Silchar kan zin chang Silchara Hmar unau kan hmupui remchang hai ka hrilpui hlak bawk a. Hmar Welfare Society, Guwahati le HSA Jt. Hqrs., Guwahati haiin HMAR LANGUAGE: RENAISSANCE AND PROSPECT ti thupui hmanga seminar an buotsai haia ka thang ve huna hai, remchanga ka hriet hai ka hrilpui hlak bawk. Hi seminar, Guwahati hmuna July 2, 2013 a nei tum khawmin Hmar tawng ziek le hmang tienga ei suong vawr, Pu L. Ruoivel Pangamte khawm ka hrilpuia tha a ti thu a mi hrila ka lawm khawp el. Chuong lai huna chun Linguistic Seminar nei chu Hmar Inpui khawmin a thuphungin tha an ti taa, kei hi Organizing Secretary dinga an mi propose thu khawm ka hriet ta bawk. Seminar neina ding hmun hin Aizawl, Hmarkhawlien le Kaziranga National Park (Assam) hai dapkuol a nia chu a remnawna le duthu sam tawk nawna a um neu neu pei leiin tuta hmun hi sukthluk a ni lem tah a nih.

Mawphurna la hmasatu dinga ruot kei, Diphu (Karbi Anglong)-ah ka uma, HI President le Gen. Secretary hai Imphal le Churachandpur-ah an uma, an hun tamtakhai an inluma um lovin an inzin kuol deu zing bawk. Chuongchun, hun a liem hrat khawp ela, ei Hmar Inpui thuoitu lien pahni hai chu hrillo, kei ve bawk khawm buoina dang dangin a mi chim buoi leiin a hun le program hai sukfel mumal theilovin a um zinga. Hieng vel lei khawm hi a ni el dim aw HLS thuoitu hai le fumfe taka inbiek a um naw nasan kha (!) ti dam ka ngaituo vel mei mei. Chu chu thudang ni ta sien. Hmar literature a hin kan hnik ve na a la sawt nawh. Kan hnik tan dan le tuchena ka ngaimaw hai hang hrildawk veng ka tih.

2010 khan Assam University, Silchar (AUS) a academics tienga thuneitu insangtak, Academic Council meeting ah ka thang ve a. Chu huna chun ka lo hriet lawk der lovin Hmar language chu MIL subject dinga Undergraduate (BA) level-a pawm a ni ding thu agenda ah a lo uma. Mak ka tia, ka phur bawk. Kei a Hmar um ve sun chu tawng ngailovin AUS a College Development Council Director chun a hung hrilfie zung zunga, kan pass ta a. Meeting zo hotel ka pan lai chun, Janata College, Kabuganj, Silchara Asst. Professor sin thawa um, hotupa Lalzawmlien ka zuk phone a, ama chun hi mi hung panin, hi chungchanga hma an lo lak dan hai a mi hril hrieta. Lawm a um takzet.

Chuongchun 2011 a inthawk chun Hmar subject chu B.A. inchuklai haiin M.I.L. subject ah ei hung nei tan tah a. College thenkhata, abikin G.C. College, Silchar ah inchuktirtu an um naw chun Hmar (MIL) subject lak phallo an lo tia. A tawi zawngin tlawmngaiin mi ruoi a hung ni tah. A hlaw ding pek a hung tula, Assam ram hmun dang danga organization lai a hlaw ding dawl a nia; Dr. Rochunga Pudaite le HSA Gen. Headquarters khawmin thla 2/3 hlaw ding khawm an hung tum ve thu mi hril ka hriet. Chuong sa khawm chu a hlaw ding chu pek zing a hung harsa a. Hlaw mumal pek theilo chang a hung uma. Pawi tak a nih. Hmar M.I.L. ei nei sun hi iengtinam sukhringin sukhmasawn ei ta (?), ti chun ka lungril a hung hluo lien ta em em el a nih. Ka hriet suol naw chun Assam University hnuoia college hai lai – G.C. College, Haflong Govt. College, Diphu Govt. College, Nehru College (Pailapool), Karimganj College (hi hi ka hriet chieng naw tlat) a hai BA level-ah Hmar (MIL) subject latu an um tawl. G.C. College ah an tam tak. Chu khela Class X – XII ah hienghai po baka hin Junior College iemani zat zeta Hmar (MIL) lak thei a ni bawk. Hienghai inchuktirtu ding post siem a ni si naw a, inchuktir lo hlak chun an Hmar tawng ziek hai chu tiem a harsa nuom vieu el bawk si.

Kei literature mi lo hin hienghai hi ka lo invawi ve rak rak hlak a nih. Hi lei hin Hmar tawng chungchanga inrawnkhawm hi pawimaw ka hung ti ve ta em em el a nih. Kei ka khata ka thaw thei a ni si nawh. Hmar Welfare Society, Guwahati le HSA Jt. Hqṭs, Guwahati haiin hma an hung lak dam kha lawmum ka ti nasa. Sienkhawm an fepui dan a danglam deu leiin Hmar tawng thua inrawnkhawmna huopzau lema nei chu makmaw ah ka ngai tah a nih.

Hieng zing lai hin Bhasa Research and Publication Centre, Baroda haiin India rama tawng dang dang hai lakhawma lekhabua sut pei an tum laiah Assam rama tawng dang dang nei hai tawng chanchin ziek an tumna ah Guwahati University ah Workshop an neina-ah fiela ka um angin April, 2012 khan ka zu thang ve a. Chutaka chun assignment – Hmar tawng chungchang ziek ding – an mi pek ta a. Chutaka ziek ding tul hai chu, ‘History of Hmar language’ ti dam, ‘brief grammatical structure’ ti dam, ‘one or two songs’, ‘one or two stories’ ti dam le ‘domain’ (tawng hmangna hmun tlangpui) hai dam an nih. Hi hi ka hang buon tan chun kei ME school-a Hmar tawnga inchukpa khawm hi ei tawng chungchang hi ka lo hriet naw nasat zie ka hriet suok nasa hle a. English medium a tan chawt el hai lem chun an hriet naw awm tak a nih. Hi lei hin iengtinam anni hai hin ei Hmar tawng hi thiem tak tak thei an ta, Class-X, XII le B.A. haiah Hmar (M.I.L.) subject ah mark hmu tha vieu hai sien khawm an thiem tak tak ka ring thei nawh. Hi lungril put hin Hmar Inpui President, John Pulamte le telephone le email in kan inbiekpaw deu char chara, HI Executive Committee khawmin an ngai pawimaw leiin President pu ngei khawm ka umna Diphu-ah Sept. 21, 2013 khan ruol dang pahni le an hung ngat a nih; Diphu-a Karbi Anglong Hmar Association (KAHA), HYA, HSA thuoituhai le inpawl tlangna hun tha kan hmang bawk a. Program ka zuk duon sin laia Hmar tawng ziek dana ei inthuruolnawna deu thenkhat, HLS in a pawmlo, ‘t’ hnuoia sunhang um aia ‘tr’ hmang chungchang le ‘aw’ tluk tawia ‘o’ hmang chungchanga chu hrilsa thain kan ngaia.

(HLS thuoituhaiin ‘thuthlukna um ta sa’ a ni leiin hrillang sin khawm a tul tanaw nia an ngai khawm awm lem chu kan ti tho);

Chu bakah chun, ‘z’ le ‘j’ hmangna khawm chingfel ngai niin anlang a. Hawrawp inkawp dang hieng CH le NG lamtawi ziek dan le a dang dang zau taka hril tlangna hun remchang hi seminar hin a mi pek beisei sa a ni bawk.

Prokram kan riruong mek lai anthawk, Hmar Inpui President chun a lungril hung sengin hienga ei inko khawm ta si si hin chu Hmar hai pawimaw dang, fak-le-dawn, inchukna le politics tienga ei ngirhmun le hmalak pei dan ding hrilsa na hun nei thei dinga hun siem a hung rawt nawk ta peia, a tha ta hlei hlei. Abikin pawlitiks tienga tutaka Mizoram, Manipur le Assam (NC Hills le Barak Valley) haia Hmarhai ngirhmun hi inhrilhriet le inrawnkhawm pawimaw takzetin ka hriet a; hi hmaa 1950s-60s lai Hmar District nawr kha ei tharthaw ding am ti hai dam ngaidan tha tak tak nei haiin hmaisana ei hriltlang na remchang a ni ding zie hai ngaituo in hi seminar hi a buoipuitu laia thang ve khawm ka phurna a nih.

Thei hram sien chu a tu tu khawm inthuruol takin thang tlang thei inla chu ka va nuom de aw!
