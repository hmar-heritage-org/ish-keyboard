PRESS

STATEMENT

Ahmasan, Hmar mipui le Hmar hnam thuoitu hai popo chunga HMAR NATIONAL ARMY (HNA) chun lawmthu a hril a, kan Pu. Lalthangsang Intoate (L) President HNA, nunrawng taka a hringna lak a a umna chungchang-a dawthei taka kan hrat tawk nawna hai khawm mi hriethiem a in mi sawmdawlna popo a HNA chun a lawm takzet bawk anih.

Dated 5th. July 2016, dar 1:30 AM vel a Pu Lalthangsang Intoate (L) President, HNA, nunrawng taka a chengna gate bula a hringna laka a umna a khan Hmar mipui chau nilovin Khawvel a thawng a, mitin a mi ba-rakhai seng anikha. Hiengang-a ei hnam sunga thil thanaw tak inthluntu le Hmar hnam tadinga thil pawi ropuitak thawtu hi, tu midang nilovin, keini, arawihai, nidinga mi ring tawk le thu leng vak le ring insiek chu Hmar mipui lai le hnam danghai lai khawma thu vuongvel lientak chu alonih.

Hiengang boruok hi alonih leiin, HNA chun theitawpin suizuina le bawzuina aneia, chutaka, thudik a hrietsuokhai le vawisunni a HNA sunga thil tlunghai chu Hmar mipui le khawvel hriet dingin ahnuoia ang khun Press Statement a siem.

Kan pu Lalthangsang Intoate (L) President, HNA, hi tu midang that nilovin Pu. HR Khawbung @ Tanpui, JMG HNA in ni 5th. July 2016, dar 1:30 am vel khan an thuruolpui mi thenkhat hai le a chengna gate bula lo inchan in hmun le hmun a nunrawng takin anlo kap hlum ta anih.

“Date 12. August 2016, Zirtawpni a inthawka Major Thanga @ Hminga le HNA Cadres popo an umna chin hriet lovin an um” ti hi thudik ani a, chin hriet lova an um hmang hri nasan chu hieng ahnuoia ang hi anih:-

Pu. HR Khawbung @ Tanpui hin kan pu Lalthangsang Intoate (L) President HNA, tuolthina a HNA hmalakna hi nasatakin a dodal (Oppose) a, Hmar mipui mi beisei ang le ditdan ang khawm a, Pu HR Khawbung @ Tanpui thang chun, HNA hin a sukpuitling in thudik hi an lang suok zonaw dinga a hriet ta leiin.

HNA in an President hi Pu. HR Khawbung @ Tanpui in a phier rukna lei-a that ngei anih ti a hriet suok ta sia, Pu. HR Khawbung @ Tanpui hin HNA a athawpuihai laka a sahimna ding hin remruotna siemin KNO chu inbikna le phaw a hmangin Major Thanga @ Hminga le HNA cadres hai chu suk bohmang danding lampui chu a hung hraw tan ta leiin.

Kan Pu Lalthangsang Intoate (L) thi na hi vawi bo a bo ding chun HNA chun a remtinaw a, tuolthat tuhai chunga an phu tawka hremna/action a lak ngei theina ding lampui a hraw mek nadinga hin um hmang hri hi tul a ahriet leiin.

Hieng a chunga tarlang a um ang khin, HNA chu bo nawni a, bo ngai bawk nawni, a lampui hraw ding chu a la hung hraw pei ding anih ti Hmar mipui popo kuoma a hung puong nghal bawk a. Kan Pu Lalthangsang Intoate (L) President HNA, nunrawng taka thattu, Pu. HR Khawbung @ Tanpui le an thuruolpuihai chunga khawm hin hun pek anni sunga thaw ding hril a an um anga anthaw naw chun an chunga Action lak ni’ng atih ti Hmar mipui hriet in HNA chun thutiem a siem nghal bawk.

Dated, Hmarrunpui, the 17th.August 2016 (Sgt. Major. EMMANUEL @ SAM)

TOWN COMMANDER
