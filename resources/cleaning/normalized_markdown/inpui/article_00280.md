Mihriem taphawtin hi khawvel a ei damlai ni a ei pumpel theilo thil

pathum a um a. Chuonghai chu Sakhuo (eini tading chun Kristienna), Fak-le-dawn

le Pawlitiks an nih! Ei damsung hi thil pathum hai hin a mi del naw thei nawh.

“Ka nghawk tah, ka thang ta nawh, ka thang nuom nawh, ka pei ta nawh”, ti a

pumpel theilo an nih. Chuleiin hi thil pathum thu a Bible inchuktirna chu hriet

le zawm ding a inchukchieng khawm a tul hle.

Hieng thil pathum ei hril lai a hin 1985 a lo indin tah, Kum 29-na hmang

mek. “Thiltha Thaw, Nundan Mawi, Tlawmngaina”, ti Thupui a neitu Sietni le

thatni a kan bel lai kan suong lungpui, Hmar Youth Association (HYA) khawm hi a

thangsa ve zing a nih. HYA Thupui hi ei Bible mi’nchuktirna tam tak le inmil em

em, Kristien nun dan ding tak, Luka 10:30-36 a Samari mithapa mizie dam, Galati

6:2 a “In rikphurhai chu insawk tuo ro...” a mi ti dam, Rom 12:15 a Lawmhai chu

lawmpui a, taphai chu tap pui dinga a mi’nchuktirna hai popo le hin an kalna

hmu ding a um naw a. An inmil thlap lem a nih. HYA le Kristienna hi an kal ang

elin mi tamtakin ei ngai el hlak hi chu a pawi tak zet! Kohran khawmin a tumin

sie ei tum tlat hlak hi thil mak a nih! Sietni le thatni haia hin HYA bovin ei

um thei der naw zie chu in hril hran khawm ngai derloa a hrietu seng ei nih.

HYA chu Kohran mi-le-sa, Kristienna le inkal deu anga ei hmu nasan,

siemthat ding chi khawm chu um ve ngei a tih. A iengpo khawm lo ni ta sien, ei

renga ei mawphurna a ni ve tlat leiin HYA a hin Kristien sakhuomi le Kohran

mi-le-sa a challanghai khawm hin rawngbawlna tho a nih, ti hriein in hmang ve

hlak inla chu ei thaw ding hrim ei thaw a nih, ti ei hriet thar nuom a um. Hnam

le HYA a dam naw chun Kohran le Kristienna, Mimal nun, Khawtlang nun khawm a

dam bik ding am? ti hi ei ngaituo nawn rawp a hun ta hle. HYA hi iengtik lai

hun a khawm Tawngtaina a ei san rawp hi Kristien hai mawphurna khawm a nih. A

chau ei ti a ni chun eini khawm ei chau tina a nih. Tukhawm intum bik ding an

um nawh!

Young Lushai Association (YLA), Tuta huopzau lema Young Mizo Association

(YMA) ti a ei hung ko tah hung intan dan tak la hang hrilsa vak inla, YMA le

Kristienna inkungkai inthukzie chu indin an rawt zan June 3, 1935-a inhmukhawm

hai a inthawk ringawt khawm hin an lang chieng vieu awm ie. Hi zan hin Zosap 3,

Mizo Pastor 1, Upa 2, Kohran hotu dang 4, Pi Zaii Bangla-ah inhmu khawmin pawl

indin an rel tah a ni kha. YMA hming hmasa YLA phuoktu khawm Zosap Rev. David

Edward (Zorema pa) a ni a. A tira YLA um san, an hmathlir chu fir tak a nih ti

an Dan zam a hin an lang chieng hle- Tlangval haiin an hun awlhai a tha tienga

an hmang a, an nun danhai an dik tieng le a hrisel zawnga an nei theina dinga

infuina a nih. Um dan iengkimah Kristien nun dan zuiin Kristien Association

angin a um ding a nih. Isu nungchang ang inentir a tum tak a nih. Hi

Association hin insawiselna hai, hriltlangna hai le lemchang hai ngaituong a

ta, Hockey, Football, Badminton, etc hai nei ning a tih. Insung luma infiemna

le lekha tiem dinghai khawm nei ning a tih... YLA Thupui tak- Kristien nun dan

ngaisangtu tuelkhawm hi pawl a hin thang thei an tih... Zu dawn pumin tukhawma

YLA hmunah amani, inkhawmna ah a thang ding a ni nawh... Mi nungchang

mawilo le tawng tlahawl chu member an ni thei nawh... Hieng danhai hi hi pawl

thatna le felna humhalh theina dinga siem a nih ti ei hmu thei a, Thuoitu

hmasahai khawm Kohran mi, rawngbawlnaa inpekna inthuk tak tak nei, ram le hnam

hmangaitu indik tak an ni pei a nih! YLA/YMA indintuhai hin hi pawl le

Kristienna hi an hmet kawp hne hle. Fe hrang thei ah an ngai naw niin an lang.

Hienglai hun a hin Kristien khawm an la ni vawng naw leiin ringnawtuhai hipna

le Isu Krista la pawm chielo hai khawmin an nunphung neka tha (Christian Social

and Ethical Values) an inchuk ve theina dinga an ngaituo a ni hmel a. A nina tak

a chun Chanchintha hrilna hmangruo a dingin hmang an tum niin an lang.

Kum 1954-a Kristian Thalai Pawl/Kristien Thalai Pawl (KTP) indin a ni

hma khan Kohran le Kristien in ti haiin YMA a hmu le ngaidan dan chu tuta KTP

ei hmu ang hi ni elin an lang. Chun, Kohran hnuoia Thalai Pawl ringum taka ni

dingin a dittawk el niin an lang bawk. Amiruokchu, khawtlang le hnam thil hriet

thiemna tienga hmasawnna a hung um peia kohran mal huongsung hi a lo chin tan

tah ning a ta. Thuoituha’n YMA fedan le ni dan an lo in duong sa chu a hung hun

tawk ta naw tan tah a. Kohran le YMA inkarah khi ni a hang hril theilo a hung

um tah a nih. YLA indin tir a inthawkin YLA Chanchin bik ziekna Thubel ti

Kristien Tlangau a belsa zie a lo ni hlak a. 1954 a inthawk chun Kristian Tlangau

a chun Thubel chu insuo nawk a ni ta ngai nawh. Hi chunchang thu hi Dr.

Sangkima chun hiengang hin a hril, “... Kohran le YMA inkara chun ‘Khi’ a hung

um a, chu ‘Khi’ chu a zau tiel tiel a, a tawp a chun Kohran le YMA chu an in

lak hrang ta nghe nghe a nih. An lang danin hieng hun lai a inthawk chau hin a

nih YMA hin ‘Social Organisation’ anga hma lak a, Mizo popo hai inlengna dinga

ke a pen tan chau na chuh! Kohran le YMA inkara inkhikna san hi thil dang tam

tak baka kum 1946 a ‘Political Party’ indin a a hung um kha niin an lang. Chun,

kohran thununna a inthawka intal suok hrim hrim nuomna khawm a um niin an lang

bawk”. An dintu kohran (Presbyterian/Synod) leh thilthaw le hmalakna haia tuta

hma angin fe kawp ta naw sienkhawm, Kristienna le YMA chu in rem tak le

indittuo takin a fe kawp pei a nih ti chu YMA hmalakna popo thlirin chieng

takin an lang.

lai hun a khawm Kristienna le inkallo, HYA Thupui- "Thiltha thaw, Nundan

mawi, Tlawmngaina" hi chawisang zing ei tiu. HYA dam zing raw se. HYA le

Kristienna/Kohran hi iengtik lai khawm the hran nawng ei tiu. Kohran le Pumkhat

a ngir tlat ding ei nih. Pathien thu a kal nawh. Khawvel a ei um sung

Sakhuo/Kristienna, Fak-le-dawn le Pawlitiks bovin ei um thei nawh...

lai, kan suong lungpui HYA hi Vul Zing Raw Seh!
