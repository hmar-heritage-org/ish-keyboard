CCpur:

16-Nov-13, Zanita inthawka a bikin North East State sunga chenghai Chi(Salt) bo inlau leiin mipui ei buoi mup mup a, chuong lai zing chun Vai Mahajon haiin pawisa anlo ham ṭawk hman bawk. State tin Sawrkar a sukbuoi nasa hman hle, Churachandpur District sunga cheng mipui hai khawm nasa takin anlo buoi ve hman a nih. Ṭhenkhat dawr thaw haiin an dawra ding chi anlo khar ṭawk ṭawk a, Sipai hai hlaka Chi(Salt) a man pangai neka tama zawr zawr man ding tiin thusuok an siem bawk leiin pawisa tam tak taka lo khar sâwngtu haiin an kharna rate neka inhnuoia zawr sâwng nawk dân an hriet ta bawk si naw leiin mipui a sukbuoi pha hle.

A hnuoia thlalak hai hi Churachandpur District sunga mipui Chi(Salt) bo inlau leia an buoina chu a nih.
