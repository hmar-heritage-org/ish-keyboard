By Pu L.KEIVOM*, Inpui Columnist

Language is the blood of the soul into which

thoughts run and out of which they grow.

-Oliver Wendell Holmes.

Khawvela hin ṭawng hrang hrangin pung tieng nekin tlawm tieng a pan pei lem a nih. Zo hnathlakhai lai khom pung nekin a kiem pei ding a nih. Chu lampui chu pumpel thei a ni nawh.

February 2002 khan UNESCO huoihotnaa mi thiem thlang khawmin khawvela ṭawng hrang hrang ngirhmun thlirna an buotsai lekhabu, Atlas of the World Languages in Danger of Disappearing ti chu Paris-ah an tlangzar a. Hi hi kum 1996-a an ripawt ennonna le thlir zauna a nih. An thil hmu chu a tawi zawngin hi hi a nih: Tu hin khawvelah ṭawng hrang hrang 6000 chuong a um a, 3000 vel chu thihmun suola um an nih. Tuta anga thil a fe pei chun, kum 2050 hin khawvela ṭawng zaa sawmkuo (90%) vel chu thi hmin der tah dingin an ring. An sut dan chun, ṭhang ṭhum liem tah sung khan ṭawng 200 chuong dai a thi hmin hman tah a nih. Ramsa an bo hrat let hni le vate an bo hrat let liin ṭawng a bo hrat lem.

Hi Atlas hi ennonin an siem ṭha pei a. Febraury 17, 2009-a a thar an tlangzara chun India rama ṭawng thi meka ngai 196 lai Zo hnathlak ṭawng hrang hrang 13 a ṭhang sa a, chuonghai laia a tam tak chu Hmar hnathlak pahnamhai ṭawng a nih. Chu chun i naah inneina dar am a vuok vuina dar?

UNESCO chun ṭawng bo mekhai chi ngain a ṭhe a. 1. Him ta lo (unsafe): Naupangin an la hmang theia chu dannaranin insung chauha an hmang. 2. Derthawng hle tah (definitely endangered): Naupangin insunga khom piengpui ṭawng anga an inchuk ta lo. 3. Nasa taka derthawng tah (severely endangered) : Pitar/putarin an la hmang, nu le pain an la hriet thei, an nauhai ruok chun an hriet ta lo. 4. Thihmun suola derthawng (critically endangered): Pitar/putar chauin an hmang le hriet. 5 Thi hmin tah (extinct): A hmang um ta lo.

Hi thu hi ka lekhabu ziek ZORAM KHAWVEL-4 ah ka chai hrep tah a. Chu taka chun German Language Society chanchinbu 'Muttersprache' (March 1996) a an hril dan ka tarlang a. An hril dan chun, tuta kum za hung ding sunga hin ṭawng la dam sun khom zaa sawmkuo vel thi hmin an ta, thi el lo dinga an ring chu ṭawng 300 vel chauh a nih. Nga lienin ngate an dol ang hin, ṭawng tenauhai chimraltu ṭawng challanghai chu English, French, Spanish, Russian le Chinese (Mandarin) an nih. Chuong laia a ngapui lien tak chu English a nih. Darwin thukhawchang ang khan a ru no no suokna khawvela hin a hrat lemin a hrat naw lem chu a hneng suok pei chu a nih.

Chu chu a ni lai zingin, ṭawng hrang hrang hi mihriem tonhriet le varna a khawl khawmhai luong khawmna a ni leiin, ṭawng ei inhmang zatin khawvelin ro hlu ei inhmang a nih. Dr. Johnson, Sapṭawng diksawnari siem hmasa taktu chun, "I am always sorry when any language is lost because languages are the pedigree of nations" a lo ti a. Ṭawng a thi zatin a hmangtuhaiin hrietna le thiemna an lo khawl khawm ta po po chu a'n hmang sa hlak a nih. Chu chu khawvel ta dingin kiemna nasa tak a nih. Chuleiin, UNESCO chun nasa takin ṭawng inhmang dinghai humhal dan a zong a, hma a lak bok a nih.

An leh, ieng leiin am ṭawng chu a bohmang el am ning a ta? Indik tak chun, ieng ṭawng khom hi a pangngai zinga dam song a um nawh. Ni tina a hmangtuhai mamaw anga insiem danglam tung pei a nih. A hmangtuhai hmasawn dan angin hma a sawn a, an inthlak danglam angin a hung inthlak danglam a, an chau pha leh a neka hrat lem ṭawngin a hung hlu suok a, an bo pha leh a bo ve el a nih. Entirnan, Italy hi hmang inla. A tira lo um Esṭuscan-hai chu Latin ṭawng hmanghaiin an hung hluo suok khan an ṭawng Etruscan khom an hluo suok a; Latin ṭawng khom a hmangtuhai khawvel inlumlet angin a hung chau tiel tiel a, Tuscanian ṭawnga inthoka hung pieng Italian ṭawngin damten a hung hluo hlan nawk a; Italian ṭawng khom hung insiem danglam peiin ieng hun amania chu ṭawng ṭhain a la hung chim nel nawk pei ding a nih. Chu lampui tho chu Hmar ṭawng khomin a hraw ve naw thei nawh.

Mihriem khawsakna dinga hmang tlak lem ṭawng ṭawng hi mihriemin ei bawr a, a hung inṭhang tuol tuol el a nih. Chuong ang ṭawng hmang tlak hriet chu thiemna nei theina hnàr, sin ṭha hmu theina tiket le khawtlang inza phurna a ni tlat leiin, sum ieng zat khom sengin nu le pa tu khomin an nauhai inchuktir ei nuom a nih. Hi lei hin thiemna ram paw theina le khawvel dawr theina dinga ṭawng filor nia tu laia ngai Sapṭawng chu khawvel pumpuiin ei inchuk tlut tlut el a nih. Chu Sapṭawng chun ni tina insiem thar pei thei dinga ṭawng tina mi a ṭûl le mamaw taphot a angsungah a lak lut pei leiin khawvela ṭawng hausa tak le hmang tlak tak a hung ni thei a nih. Sapṭawng hi khawvel hnam tin infe pawna dinga leilak a nih.

Amiruokchu, mihriem hi bei chauha hring le lungawi a ni nawh. A hlimna le lawmna, a lungkhamna le intarna, a beiseina le beidongna, a lungsenna le lungawina, a lungril ruk taka a thil invoi, a awm sunga a khawl khawm hai chu ama ṭawng thiem zawng, naupang a ni laia inthoka a hung inṭhanglienpui ṭawng ngeia hril le inzawt a nuom a, chu chu inhoi a ti bok a. Chuonga kut le ke zaizir ṭhang ṭhak loa a thaw thei chun ieng ang khomin a awm inhnok sien, bau khat a hril zatin a hung inthawl thla hok hok el a nih. Ieng angin hnam dang ṭawng inchuk thiem inla khom, ei seilienpui ei nu ṭawnga thu le hla ang tluka ei thlarau chawm tlei theitu a um nawh. Sapṭawng, Mizo-Lusei ṭawng le Hmar ṭawng hai hi ka hriet le thiem dan inang thuok vong a tih. Sapṭawng chu ka fakzongna le sinthawna ṭawng a ni a. Mizo-Lusei ṭawng chu lekhabu ka ziek rawnna tak ṭawng le thu le hlaa nasa taka ka hringnun chawmtu ṭawng a ni nawk a. Hmar ṭawng ruok chu ka nu ṭawng, sula inthoka ka hung seilienpui, ka thisen le inzom tlat, pi le puhai le mi ṭingmittu, ka Pathien ka biekna ṭawng, van chena ka som ding, 'ka ṭawng' a nih.

Sienkhom, chu ka nu ṭawng chu dam khawsuok a tim? ti chu zawna don harsa tak a nih. Thu ka hrilna hmun iemani zatah Hmar ṭawng hi kum sawmhni neka tam liem tah khan a thi ta a, fan invir vut vut, electric ei that hnung khoma iemani chen a lan vir ang mei mei a ni thu ka hril tah hlak a. Chu chu ka hmu dan char a ni hrim a. Hi thudik, kha tak el hi ngaithlatuhai chun fak harsa an ti a, soi le lem thlâk lem chu harsa an ti zuol. Mizo Students Union-in khuolliena an mi fiel leia Pune-a kan inzin ṭumin Union Biblical Seminary-a inchuklaihai leh kan titi tlangnaah hi thu bok hi ka hril a. Lem harsa an ti el chauh khom ni lovin an lung a sen nuom hielin ka hriet.

Hi thu hi eini rawi, mani ṭawng ngaina le hmangai inti, amiruokchu a tak ngeia a damna dinga iengkhom thaw si lo, ni tina a thlân chotuhai chona (challenge) dingin a ṭha leiin hril hril chi niin ka hriet. Ieng leia Hmar ṭawng hi a thi tah anga ei hril am a na? A san tam tak lai pahni chauh entirna dingin hang hril ei tih.

Pakhatna, ṭawng hring chu ni tinin a'n ṭhang a, a ni telin pung deu deu hlak a, Hmar ṭawng ruok chu a ni telin a kiem lem. Kum sawmruk vel liem taa Rev. Thangngurhai hun laia Hmar ṭawng an hriet le hmang thiem zatve khom kha ṭhangtharhai hin an hriet ta nawh niin an lang. Lekha thiema ei tiem chin lai khom hin Hmar ṭawnga lekhathon phek khat khom him ronga ziek thei ei vang tah êm êm. Chu chu inzaka lak nek hmana indunaa nei pha khom ei um hielin an lang. Sapṭawnga tlawmte ei inpawt thei chun mani ṭawng thiem naw inla khom mi taka inngaina lungril ei nei niin an lang. Sapṭawng thiem sia mani ṭawng ei thiem naw chun ei ṭawng chu ei ngaithà tina a nih. Chuleiin, ei ṭawng vuitu chu eini rawi hi ei ni a, mi dang intum thei ding ei ni nawh., ṭawng hring chu ni tinin a'n ṭhang a, a ni telin pung deu deu hlak a, Hmar ṭawng ruok chu a ni telin a kiem lem. Kum sawmruk vel liem taa Rev. Thangngurhai hun laia Hmar ṭawng an hriet le hmang thiem zatve khom kha ṭhangtharhai hin an hriet ta nawh niin an lang. Lekha thiema ei tiem chin lai khom hin Hmar ṭawnga lekhathon phek khat khom him ronga ziek thei ei vang tah êm êm. Chu chu inzaka lak nek hmana indunaa nei pha khom ei um hielin an lang. Sapṭawnga tlawmte ei inpawt thei chun mani ṭawng thiem naw inla khom mi taka inngaina lungril ei nei niin an lang. Sapṭawng thiem sia mani ṭawng ei thiem naw chun ei ṭawng chu ei ngaithà tina a nih. Chuleiin, ei ṭawng vuitu chu eini rawi hi ei ni a, mi dang intum thei ding ei ni nawh.

Pahnina, ṭawng sukhringtu le sukhausatu chu a hmangtu mihriemhai an nih. A hmangtuhai an hringa an chakvak po leh an ṭawng hmang khom a hringin a chakvak el a nih. Chu umzie inril nawk chu, ngaituona thatho nei mi le hmang mi, thil thar zong suok mi le chîng suok mi an nih tina a nih. Mihriemin hmasawnna kalbi a phak hla po leh a ngirhmun phak chin hrilna dingin ṭawng thar a ser suok rawn a, hma a sawn nasa po leh a ṭawng hmangruo khom a pung el a nih. Eini rawi ngirhmun teh iem a na? Thil thar hmu suok chu hril lo, thil hlui vong ṭha pei khom ei vang êm êm. Ei pi le puhaiin arbawm ta dan teknawlawzi an thiem khela khan kal khat khom ei la pen naw khom a ni el thei. Ei ṭawng ziek dan phuisui lem le ṭha lem zong suok tum nek hmanin mi zong suok ta sa khom hmang pei lo le hmang nachâng hre lo, inhnawmna ngaia inhnawm tum zing ei kat nukin an lang. Mithi ṭhung khawm ei ang takin ka hriet. A hmangtuhai an hringa an chakvak po leh an ṭawng hmang khom a hringin a chakvak el a nih. Chu umzie inril nawk chu, ngaituona thatho nei mi le hmang mi, thil thar zong suok mi le chîng suok mi an nih tina a nih. Thangngur le a hun laia mi hluihaiin hla ropui tak taka an min awi hnung, kum sawmnga sung hin literechar tling khop, bel chieng dawl hla hring le thu hring ei awm sunga inthokin ieng zat am ei phu suok ta a? Churachandpur-a ka'n zin ṭumin R. Tawna Khobungin Lenruol Hlabu sut hnina a buoipui lai a ni leiin inchuon sa ding hla thar a lak khawmhai chu lo edit-pui dingin a mi fiel a. Ei hla thar a tam lem chu thluk nei an ni leia hlaa tiem sa ni tah mei mei niin ka hmu a. Hi ṭum hin a nih ei kawrong takzie dam, ei inhmang thuk takzie dam, ei kalchar pawhleu le khawthlang kalchar pawhleu inkara boruok mong nei lova inleng ei lo tam takzie dam ka hmu suok zuol chu ni. Puithu ngiela an siem kaset ṭhenkhat hi hang ngaithla inla, ei ṭawng ei ngaitha takzie le bohmangna tuipui ei pal inthuk takzie mi hriltu an nih. Thu le hla hlutna le a buk rikzie inkhina chu a se êk tam zawng (quantity) ni loin a ṭhatzie inhlirna (quality) a ni lem.

Hnam pakhata insâl ve lêmin thla kip suok chanchinbu pakhat takngiel khom ei sukhring ṭha thei naw hin ei ngirhmun a tar lang chieng hle. Kohran chanchinbu pahni pathum, thu le hlaa hnuchâk invoi ti hriet ngawi ngawi, tiem tlâk anni lei ni lova pawl chanchinbu an ni leia hring mei mei, ei nei ṭawk a. Keini rawi hun laia chanchinbu inzom zata suok hlak Inchuklai Nun hi a dam hun nekin a thi hun sung hi a sawt lem a. A'n khat tawka suok hlak le ei suongtawlol Nuhmei Magazine hlak chu a buoipuituhai chawibang ruolin a chawibang amani ding, a hmel hmu ding a um ta nawh. Iemani chen Sawrtui magazine chu mi lawm hlaw khopin a hung inlar hman a, sienkhom a hming chawina Sortui Rakam Neilal le Ṭuoni kara inhmangaina ang elin a hlamzui nawk tah khom a hoi khop el, a hmel hmu ding a vang tah. Tleirawl tha inthak anga hung inper suok hlut Hmathlir hlak chu Saikot damdawi rimin sawt naw teah a ur chau nawk der bok a. RPC-hai hmalaknaa insuo Zawllung magazine hin iem a chang pei ding maw! Kum 25 zet lo suo tah, kar tin suok Delhi Thurawn hi ei chanchinbua hring ṭha tak le tiem tlak tak chu a ni el tah awm. Hi khom hi mi tlawmte tlawmngaina leia hring mei mei a nina chin a um a, inchuklaihaia inthokin artikul hmu ding a tlawm a, an ziek sun tam lem hlak Sapṭawng a ni nawk nghal. Inbieknaah ei ṭawng la hmang zing inla khom ṭhalaihai lai Hmar ṭawng nekin Sapṭawngin hmun a chang rawn lem dai tah ti a chieng. Hi hi Hmar ṭawng a thi hratzie inkhina chieng tak pakhat chu a nih.

Hmar ṭawnga lekhabu ei tiem rawn tak chu inzakum khopa inphuoia an min letpek Baibul hi a nih. Ṭhangtharhaiin Hmar ṭawng an thiem naw deu deu pei khom hi a kaikuongpa pakhat chu ei Baibul inleta Hmar ṭawng indik lo ei hmang ṭeuh hi a nina chin umin an lang, ei lekhabu tiem rawn tak a ni tlat a. Hi lei hin a nih lo thei loa Delhi tlanga inthoka siem phuisui tuma hma an lak pha hiel chuh. Hi hin thlan muol pan mek ei ṭawng hi a kei tho thei hram ei beisei a nih. Chun, sakhuo tieng thu le hla hi ei hmasawnna tak niin ei hril hlaka chu ei awm thu suok lekhabu tiem tlak pakhat khom ka la hmu ve nawh. Ṭawng danga inthoka ei inlethai khom hi, ka tiem ve phak chin chu, mang sietna khopa turu a tam. Ei pulpithai hi ṭawng ei suosamna, 'Ṭawngthatzawl' ei inchangtir vong tah. Na tak a nih.

Ei ṭawng vui liem mektu chu eini ngei ei nih ti hi ei hriet chieng a ngai. Eini ṭawnga chanchinbu amanih lekhabu sut rêng rêng inchawtu ni lova mi inchawk sa tiem tumna lungril put dam hi ṭawng vui liemna lampui hnai tak pakhat a nih. Hi hi ei pasiet lei a nina chin um sienkhom a'n dik chie nawh. A san chu, lekhabu inchawkna dinga cheng khat khom ei inro laiin taksa le thlarau ta dinga ṭangkaina nei der lo kuva, sada, ziel le kamcherek mei mei inchawkna ding chun sum ei nei tawl tlat si a nih. Ei ṭawng hi vui liem song ei nuom a ni ngot si naw chun ei nungchang thlak a, ei insiem thar a ṭûl makmaw.
