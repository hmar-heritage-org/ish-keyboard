By Alan Famhoite

Zani zana an released chau la lum hlawt hlawt "DAMLAI THLIPUI" (Storms of Life) Pi Lalronghak w/o Dr.J.L.Songate in a ziek, Diamond Offset publication chu kan chawk ve a, a bu hi vawisun ka tiem suok vawng a, Bung-15 umin inchawk man a um kher el.

Ei hringnuna hin thlipui chi hran hran ei tuok hlak a, Chuonghai chu Pasietna Thlipui dâm, Lungngana Thlipui dâm, Thlêmna Thlipui dâm, Muolphona Thlipui dâm, Thlaphângna Thlipui dâm le FamThlipui hai hi an ni a, Chuong ei tuok huna tuordan ding hai le ei mawlsawmna dawng hai hi lawmna chang hriet a pawimaw ziet (Bung-9) hai tulai ṭhangthar/ṭhalai context deua hrilin a ziek chipchier theim hle, Lawmthu hril na ding tamtak ka nei zie a min hriet suok tir pha bawk a nih. Ei malsawm dawng tamtak hai hi ani ding reng, ei dawng ding reng anga ngaina nasatak ṭhalai lai a pieng ta khawm hi tuta lekhabu hmang hin ṭhangthar tamtak, Pathien kuoma le Nu le Pa, Pi/Pu hai kuoma lawmthu hril ei bat zie min hriettirna ni mawl sien ka nuom.

A pa (father) inchuktirna Bung-10 ah Point-16 zetin a ziek a, chuong hai khawm chu mitin hriet dingin a ṭha kher el, Social response le insunga Hospitality hai anni a, chun mi biek dan thiem tum ding ti dam an nih. Insung tina apply seng dingin a ṭha kher el.

Chun Bung-13 ah mani insung senga Tlawmna nun nei pawimaw zie uor takin a hril a, "Ei Hnam nunphung hnungtieng nasa taka an tawlna khawm hi HYA lungril putna nun a tlâwm ta lei a nih." a ti hiel bawk a nih.

Tuta a chunga ka hung ziek lang hai khi a bua ṭha kati bik hai tina anni chuong nawh, A bu(Book) hi a rengin a Bung khatna a inthawk Bung-15 na chen hi a man le en min chun a ṭha hlie hlie el a nih.

Ala nei ve lo hai khan inchaw sup sup ro inchawk man aum hle.
