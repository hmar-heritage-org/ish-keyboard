Mipui Kuoma Hrilfiena

March 26, 2015

March 22, 2015 zana Mr. Lalthuthlung Hmar chunga keini HPC-D in Action kan lak chungchang a hiengang hi thil umdan a nih. Hi chungchang thu a hin pawl le mimal tukhawmin sawisel thei naw ni hai, Ama mimal thil suksuolna chunga kan thaw ding tak kan thaw a nih. Dan le dun kan nei a, ama khawmin a thu tiem a bawsiet leia a chunga hi thil hi hung tlung ta a nih.

Ama hi hnam inthuruolna suksie zawngin a chang rawp hlak a, Barak Valley Hill-Tribes’ Development Council (BVHTDC) Town Election hun a hai HSA a a nina changchawiin hi BVHTDC a hin Council Chairman dam dan naw ang takin an chu a, 1996 laia HPC Returnees hai Rehabilitation Case le Memorandum hai khawm a lo ziek pui hlaka, ama ngeiin hiengang thil hi thaw ta ngailo ding le Council Chairman an chuna hi tawpsan ta dinga an tiem hnung khawma ama intiemna ngei bawsiein hma a la lak zawm nawk pei leia Action hi lak a nih.

“Hi ka thilthaw suolna, kut ka tuorna a hin lungawinawna ka nei nawh, a

ul chun hnam a khawm kan pek nuom, thawsuol ka neia ka hming an lang nawk a ni chun keiin mawphur ka tih.” ti a suoi ama sie-le-

ha hrietna le ama kut ngei a 22/3/2015 ni a suoi kei a nih.

Hi Action lak zana kut tuor mi 3 hai laia ama hi a tuor na naw tak lem a ni laia kut natak tuor anga khel let dera suklanga um hi thudiklo a nih. Hi pawl hmalakna kal zawnga che hlakhai chu tuelkhawm nisien Action an phu tawk pek ni pei an tih.

Ram le hnam tadinga in aihnaphapui;

(L.K. Hmar)

PRO, HPC-D

Gen. Hqrs., Sinlung
