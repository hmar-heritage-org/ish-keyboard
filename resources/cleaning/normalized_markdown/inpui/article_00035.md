Nov 3, 2009:

Senvon khawtlang po po inzawmkhawm chun Manipur Simthlanga Chanchintha hung lut kum 100 tlingna chu kum thar January 21-23 khin ropui taka hmang dingin an in singsa mek.

Kum 100 tling hriet zingna dingin Lunghai phun dingin an keikhawm vawng ta a; Senvon a Committee indin khawm dittawk el lovin sin hai a fe tluong theina ding le hma lak a hrat nawk zuol thei na dingin Tuithaphai-ah hai chen sub-Committee an indin tah.

Hrietchieng thei china chu Centenary Hrietzingna Lung hi Pu Rev Rochunga Pudaite hawng dinga sukfel a ni tah. Speaker ding ruok chun Pu Rev Mansang Songate ning a tih.

Hi huna hin Senvon khuo suokhai hrim hrim chu inhmai um der lova thang kim seng chu committee ditdan a ni a; sienkhawm, umna a lo indar tak leiin mi hriet kim el an thei tak naw leiin, mi tin el chu ei sung-le-kuo hai le ei hriet hmai pal hai lo hril pei dinga ngen an nih.
