Rev Lalneimawi in uluk taka a buotsai “Mania Hmar |awng Inchukna” ti lekhabu thar chu zawr suok thei dinga sut zo a ni tah a. Hmar tawng inchuk nuom le thiem nuomhai ta dinga hmang tangkai thei tak ni dingin ka ring. Hmar MIL inchuktuhai ta ding lem chun an sawrtangkai zuol ka ring. A dithai chun Pine Hill Agency, Police Thana tuol, Teddim Road-ah inchawk thei zing a nih. Copy pakhat man Rs. 60/- chau a nih.
