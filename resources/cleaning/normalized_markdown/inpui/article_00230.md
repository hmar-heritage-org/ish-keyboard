Hi hla hung sûok dan: Hi hla phuoktu Tv Lalruotlien hi Pathien ram hmangai em em el, ni a hril a nih. "A thei ang anga ṭhalai pawl haia active taka inhmang hlak ani a. Nikhat chu Ṭhalai buvûok (Harvesting) huna changsuolin Motor a inthawk tla thlain Bu pâwtin a del a, chutaka inthawk chun a hung zeng (Paralyze) ṭan ta a nih," tin hi hla YouTube a upload tu hai chun an hril.

Tuhin khum chunga kum thum lai a zâl ta a, bu hai inba fawm ngaiin le chang thei talo a um a nih.

"Sienkhawm a nat khuma inthawk hin Pathienin a maksan naw a, A thil tuok hi malsawmna ah ngaiin hi hla hi a hung phuok ta a nih.A chanchin hre taphawt chun hi hla an ngai huna an ṭap naw thei nawh," ti'n an ziek bawk.
