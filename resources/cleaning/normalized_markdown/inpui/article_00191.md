HMAR STUDENTS’ ASSOCIATION

Joint Headquarters, Delhi

Regd. No. 820/67 (Manipur); 29/67 (Assam); 6/88 (Mizoram)

Dated: 16 December, 2012

THUSUOK

Independent Church of India General Assembly 63-na thurêl (Resolution) No.5 dungzuiin Delhi sûngah remchâng hmasa taka kohran pâwl bîk hminga inkhâwmna nei ta dinga thurêl hi Hmar Students’ Association, Joint Headquarter, Delhi chun ṭha a ti naw a, kan dit naw thu kan puong.

Hi thil hin hun la hung tlung pei dingah Hmar ṭawng hmang Delhia cheng hai lai Inpumkhatna, Inthuruolna, Inlainatna nasa takin a suksiet ding a ni leiin le thil dang dangah a kâkhâwk ṭhanaw tak tak a um ding a ni leiin hi thurêl hi sukpuitling lo dingin thuneituhai kan ngên takzet cheu a, hun la hung tlung pei dingah kohran dang dang hai khawmin Delhi-a mipui inpumkhatna hi mi hung suksie pek lo hram dingin kan ngen nghal bawk cheu.

Hi thu le hin Delhia chêng Hmar mipuihai tukhawm lungril chi-ai a buoi lo dingin kan ngên bawk.
