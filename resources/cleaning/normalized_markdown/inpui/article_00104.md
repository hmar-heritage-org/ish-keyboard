4:14 ‘Saffron’ tia sie hi Hebrai tong chun ‘karkom’ ti a ni a, par chi khat, a bul hlawnga inthoka hung inder suok, a par chu a then a hringduk dam, a then a eng, serthlum rong ang dam a nih.
